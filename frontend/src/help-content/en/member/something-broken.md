If something you borrowed, booked or used isn't working, tell the space from the place you used it.

## Where to find the button

- **A tool:** open **Loans**, then your loan, then **Report a problem**.
- **A machine:** open your job page and press **Report a problem with the machine**.
- **A room:** open **My bookings**, then the booking, then **Report a problem**.

## Send it

1. Press the button for the thing that's broken.
2. If there are several items, choose **Which item**.
3. Fill in **What's wrong** and **Details**.
4. Add up to six photos if they help.
5. Press **Send report**.

![Screenshot: the Report a problem form](screenshot:member-something-broken)

The space emails you when it's fixed.

> **Who can do this:** the person whose loan, job or booking it is. Borrowers without an account use their confirmed email.
