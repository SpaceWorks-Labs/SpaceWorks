## Back up and restore

1. Open **Backup & restore**. Under **This space**, press **Back up now**. A nightly backup also runs.
2. **Restore points** are labelled nightly, before a restore, or from a whole-platform backup.
3. To go back, press **Restore…** on a point, then **Preview** to see what changes, by type of record.
4. Press **Restore this space** to go ahead, or **Cancel this restore**.

![Screenshot: Backup & restore with restore points](screenshot:sm-backup-restore)

Every restore shows a preview before it changes anything, and a safety backup is taken first.

## Export your records

1. Open **Data export** and read the notice: an export is a readable copy, not a backup, and it contains members' names and contact details.
2. Press **Request export**, wait for **Ready**, then **Download**.

Only one export runs at a time.

## Space packs

Turn on **Space packs** under Features to copy your configuration (policies, templates, catalogue definitions) to another space. **Export configuration** downloads it; on the other space, paste it, press **Preview import** and then **Apply reviewed pack**. Items arrive with zero stock.

## Moving to your own server

Under **Backup & restore**, **Move to your own server** asks the platform administrator to export your space for a new server.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager. Whole-platform backups are the platform administrator's.
