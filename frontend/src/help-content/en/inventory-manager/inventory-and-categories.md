## Add an item

1. Open **Inventory** (shown as **Items**) and press **Add item**.
2. Fill in its name, category, quantity and how it's tracked: as a count, or as individual units with their own QR.
3. Choose its public visibility: whether it's listed, and whether the public sees an exact count, just a status, or nothing.
4. Save.

![Screenshot: the Add item form](screenshot:im-add-item)

Storage locations, box codes and QR codes are never shown publicly.

## Change counts

Open an item and press **Adjust quantities**. Change available, damaged or lost, and give an **Adjustment reason**. Counts can never go below zero, and every adjustment is in the audit log.

## Other item actions

**Show QR**, **Lending history**, **Move to repair shelf**, **Add to To buy** and **Archive**. Archived items leave the catalogue; restore them from the **Archived** tab. **Export CSV** and **Export Excel** download the list.

## Categories

Open **Categories** and press **Add category** (name, short name, icon, order). Use **Edit** or **Delete**.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with the inventory view sees items; Inventory Managers and the Space Manager change them.
