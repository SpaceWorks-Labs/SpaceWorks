Some events sell tickets in orders: you can buy places for friends, add extras such as a kit or a meal, and pay once.

## Buy

1. On the event page, under **Tickets**, choose how many of each ticket you want. Add any **Add-ons**.
2. Have a code for a hidden ticket? Press **Have a code?**, enter it and press **Unlock**.
3. Tick **One of these is for me** if you're going yourself.
4. Press **Review order** and check **Your order**: the booking fee, the total and the refund policy.
5. Press **Confirm order**, or **Continue to payment** to pay online.

![Screenshot: the ticket shop with Tickets, Add-ons and Your order](screenshot:member-ticket-shop)

Your places are held for about 30 minutes while you pay. If the price changes before you pay, you see the new total first.

## Name your attendees

1. Open **My orders** in your member area and pick the order.
2. Under **Places**, enter each attendee's email and press **Save**, or press **This one is mine**.
3. Each attendee gets an email. They open **Claim your ticket** and press **Claim my place**.
4. Press **Remind people to claim** for anyone who hasn't yet (once a day at most).

An unpaid order offers **Pay now** or **Abandon this order**; abandoning puts the places back on sale.

## A ticket passed to you

Open the emailed link, sign in with the address it was sent to, and press **Accept the ticket**.

> **Who can do this:** signed-in people, for events that sell tickets in orders. Refunds are decided by the organisers under the event's policy.
