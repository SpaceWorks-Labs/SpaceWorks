An ID programme is a card an organisation issues through the space: a student ID, a volunteer ID or a staff badge, for example.

## Join with a link

1. Open the join link the organisation shared. It looks like `/o/<organisation>/ids/<programme>`.
2. Sign in.
3. Answer the questions.
4. Add a photo if asked, and tick the photo consent box.
5. Press **Ask to join**.

![Screenshot: an ID programme join page with the Ask to join button](screenshot:member-id-join)

You then see **Waiting for approval**, or **Approved** with a link to **Open My cards**. Your card appears in [My cards](help:member/member-card).

## Accept an invitation

If the organisation added you, you get an email. Open its link, sign in with the same email address, and accept. Nothing is linked to your account until you accept.

> **Who can do this:** any signed-in person. Your photo is stored privately and printed only with your consent.
