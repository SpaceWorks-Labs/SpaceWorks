**Lending settings** (from the menu on the Inventory page) holds the rules every loan follows.

## What you can set

- **Loan length and renewals**: how long a loan lasts, how many renewals are allowed and for how long. Inventory Managers can always change these, unless the Space Manager locks them.
- **Borrow limits**: how many loans or items one person may hold at once.
- **Pickup times and windows**: let members choose when they'll collect, and release an accepted request that isn't collected in time.
- **Waitlist**: queue people for an item that's out and hold the next unit for the first in line.
- **Withdrawals**: let members withdraw their own request.

![Screenshot: the Lending settings dialog](screenshot:im-lending-settings)

Other lending switches need the Space Manager to give you control of lending settings; see [Settings, and who can change them](help:space-manager/settings-and-grants).

## Waive a loan fee

1. Open the request, then **Charges**.
2. Press **Waive fees**, tick the fees and give a reason.
3. Press **Waive fees** again to confirm.

Deposits are never waived. A fee that was already paid isn't reversed here; it opens a refund for staff to approve.

> **Who can do this:** Inventory Managers and the Space Manager.


## Choose what visitors see in public stats

Open **Inventory → Lending settings** and find **What appears in public stats**. Each figure or list has its own switch, including library size, availability, loans, popular tools and the optional current-loans list. Change the switches you are allowed to edit, then press **Save public stats**. The public page and TV board use the same choices.

The Space Manager grants control of these lending settings and can lock individual choices. Hidden or unavailable areas do not erase records. Borrower names still require their separate privacy opt-in; showing a widget cannot override that setting. The Space Manager controls the overall stats page, section order and period.
