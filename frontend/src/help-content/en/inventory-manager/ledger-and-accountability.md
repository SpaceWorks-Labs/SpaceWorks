## The ledger

1. Open **Ledger**: everything that's out right now.
2. Filter by **Reviewed**, **Direct** or **Self-checkout**, or tick **Overdue only**.
3. Search or sort, and **Export CSV** or **Export Excel**.

## Accountability

1. Open **Accountability**. Tabs show **All**, **Overdue**, **Problems**, **Repeat offenders** and **Restricted**.
2. Press **Open loan** on a row to see its handover and return evidence.

![Screenshot: the Accountability page with Overdue and Problems](screenshot:im-accountability)

Lost and damaged items tie back to the borrower here. A loan closed with an issue stays on their record. Borrowers without an account are shown as totals only.

## Restricting a borrower

Restricting someone stops new requests until access is restored. Only the platform administrator restricts and restores a person; ask them, with the loan and the reason.

> **Who can do this:** anyone who sees inventory reads the ledger; Accountability needs the audit action (Inventory Manager, Space Manager).
