## Decide on requests

1. Open **Bookings**, then **Requests**. Filter **Awaiting review**, **Confirmed**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled** or **All**.
2. Press **Approve**, or **Reject with reason**. The requester is told either way, so a reason is required.
3. Inside a space you can also **Cancel**, **Complete** or mark **No-show**, with a reason.

![Screenshot: the booking Requests list](screenshot:bm-requests)

## Adjust a booking

Where staff reservation tools are on, open a booking and use **Edit note and answers** or **Edit booking time**. **Undo cancellation** restores a recent cancellation. Cut-offs still apply, and no money moves.

## Money

A priced booking raises a charge when it's confirmed. On a booking's row, **Mark offline** records a payment taken at the desk, and **Waive** cancels the charge. A booking staff cancel owes a full refund, decided in **Refunds**.

> **Who can do this:** Booking Managers, the Space Manager, and resource staff granted reservation management.
