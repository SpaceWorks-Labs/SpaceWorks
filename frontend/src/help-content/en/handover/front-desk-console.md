People who only work the front desk (the **Handover** role) get a short console built around three queues. On a phone it shows as tabs along the bottom.

## Your queues

1. Open **Home**. **Your handover queues** shows **Ready to issue**, **Due returns** and **Pickups**.
2. Press a tile to open that queue.
3. In **Requests** you see **Ready for handover**, **Active loans** and **History**.

![Screenshot: the front-desk Home with the three queue tiles](screenshot:ho-home)

## The daily round

- Hand over accepted requests: [Hand over a request](help:inventory-manager/issue-a-request).
- Take returns: [Take a return](help:inventory-manager/process-a-return).
- Lend a tool on the spot: [Direct handout](help:handover/direct-handout).
- Give out finished machine jobs: [Job pickups](help:handover/job-handover).

## What the front desk can't do

Accept or reject requests, edit the inventory or manage QR labels. Holding another role as well (Inventory Manager, say) opens the full console.

> **Who can do this:** the Handover role, alone or alongside others. Every handover is recorded with the scan, the photo and your name.
