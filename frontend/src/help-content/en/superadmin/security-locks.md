title: Lock a space's security settings
summary: Hold a space's sign-in rules at a minimum it can tighten but never loosen.

1. Choose the space and open **Security**.
2. Change a setting if needed. Each change asks you to confirm your identity.
3. To hold it, add an optional note for the space and press **Lock at current value** (or **Lock on**).
4. **Remove platform lock** undoes it.

![Screenshot: a locked security setting](screenshot:sa-security-lock)

A locked setting shows the space what it allows; the Space Manager can make it stricter but not looser. Two-step sign-in for staff is a platform rule and can't be switched off by anyone. Every change is audited in the space.

> **Who can do this:** the platform superadmin locks; the space's Space Manager edits within the lock.
