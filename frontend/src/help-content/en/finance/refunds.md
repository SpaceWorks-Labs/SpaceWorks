Every refund in Space Works is a case a person decides. No money goes back automatically.

## Decide a case

1. Open **Refunds**. Filter **To decide**, **Provider refused**, **Approved**, **Declined** or **All**.
2. Open a case and read why it was opened: a member's request, a staff cancellation, a returned gift, a lowered price.
3. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**.
4. Set the **Amount to refund** and a **Note to the member**.
5. Confirm.

![Screenshot: a refund case with Approve and Decline](screenshot:fin-refund-case)

A card payment is refunded through the payment provider; if the provider refuses, the case moves to **Provider refused**. A cash payment is refunded by hand and recorded.

> **Who can do this:** the manager of the area the money belongs to, and the Space Manager. The finance view alone can read cases but not decide them.
