Some tools carry their own QR label. Where the space allows it, you check them out and back in yourself.

## Borrow

1. Open **Borrow or return a tool** (`/checkout`), or **Scan a tool** on the Inventory page.
2. Choose **Borrow**.
3. Take a photo of the tool.
4. Press **Scan QR** and scan its label.
5. If it's a kit, **Check the kit** and count the parts.
6. Press **Confirm checkout**. You see "You've got it."

![Screenshot: the Borrow or return a tool page with Borrow and Return](screenshot:member-self-checkout)

## Return

1. Open the same page and choose **Return**.
2. Take a photo and add **Return condition notes**.
3. Scan the tool and press **Confirm return**.

Something wrong with it? Press **Report a problem with this tool** on the scan panel.

> **Who can do this:** members, or, where the space allows it, borrowers without an account who confirm their email with a code. A photo is always required; returns are never refused.
