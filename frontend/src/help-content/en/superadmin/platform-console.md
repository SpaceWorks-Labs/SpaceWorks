The platform administrator (superadmin) has a separate account from everyone else, with its own sign-in.

1. Open `/admin?context=platform`. The page says **Platform console**.
2. Enter your username or email and password, and press **Sign in**.
3. Enter the code from your authenticator if you've set one up.
4. On your very first sign-in, change the generated password.

![Screenshot: the Platform console sign-in](screenshot:sa-sign-in)

Forgot the password? Use **Forgot password?**; it needs the platform's email to be set up. If email is down, the host operator can reset the password, or the authenticator, from the server. See [Health checks and logs](help:host-operator/health-and-logs).

> **Who can do this:** the platform superadmin only. Staff and members never sign in here.
