A station is a shared device at the desk. Its PIN unlocks the device; the person working it signs in, so every handover is recorded against them.

## Set up a station

1. Open **Settings**, then **Inventory**, then **Kiosk stations**.
2. Name it, choose **Handover** or **Scanner**, and press **Create station**.
3. Copy the PIN (shown once) and the station's address.
4. Later: **Rotate PIN**, **Reveal PIN**, **Disable**, or re-enable with a new PIN.

## Work a shift

1. On the device, open the station address and enter the station PIN. Press **Unlock station**.
2. Press **Start operator shift** and sign in as yourself.
3. Enter a request number and press **Load request**.
4. Scan or assign the box, take the photo and add remarks.
5. Press **Issue items** or **Return items**.

![Screenshot: a kiosk station during an operator shift](screenshot:ho-kiosk)

If the connection drops, keep working: the station queues the work, encrypted on the device, and sends it when it's back online.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager sets up stations; front-desk and inventory staff work them under their own sign-in.
