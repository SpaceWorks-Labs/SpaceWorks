Every staff role needs two-step sign-in: an authenticator app or a passkey. Member features never need it, and the rule can't be switched off.

## New staff

A person who gains a staff role sees a banner with the date their grace period ends. They press **Set it up now**, choose **A passkey** or **An authenticator app**, and follow the steps in [Two-step sign-in and passkeys](help:member/two-step-sign-in). After three days, staff screens are blocked until they enrol; their member area keeps working.

![Screenshot: the staff banner asking to set up two-step sign-in](screenshot:staff-mfa-grace)

## When someone loses their device

1. Ask them to sign in with a recovery code, if they saved one.
2. Otherwise, open **Users**, find the person and press **Reset authenticator**. Give a reason.
3. They enrol again at their next sign-in.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager (or the platform administrator) resets a staff member's authenticator. Every staff member enrols themselves.
