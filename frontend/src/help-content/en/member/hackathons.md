A hackathon on Space Works has its own pages under the event: application, team, project, help and more.

## Apply and confirm

1. Open the event and press **Apply**.
2. If you get an offer, confirm it on **Application and RSVP** before the deadline (four days unless the event says otherwise). An offer is not a confirmed place until you do.

## Build your team

1. Open **Your team**.
2. Share an invitation link with friends, or use **Team finder** to show your card and **Request to join** another team.

## Submit

1. Open **Project and files** and fill in your project.
2. The team leader presses **Submit project** before the deadline.

Submitting freezes your team and your project's content.

![Screenshot: the Project and files page with Submit project](screenshot:member-hackathon-submit)

## Get help

- **Mentor help**: choose your team and a help category, describe what you need and where you sit, then **Request help**.
- **Office hours**: press **Book for our team** on a slot.
- **Contribute**: volunteer for a role and **Apply to shift**. Hours count once an organiser verifies them.

After judging, the event's **Results** and **Gallery** pages show the projects the organisers publish.

> **Who can do this:** signed-in applicants. Mentoring, volunteering and the gallery appear only when the organisers switch them on.
