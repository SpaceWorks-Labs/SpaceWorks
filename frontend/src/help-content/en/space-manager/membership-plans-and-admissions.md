## Plans

1. Open **Members**, then **Plans**.
2. Press **Create plan**: name, amount, currency and how often it renews. Press **Save plan**.
3. Use **Activate**, **Deactivate** or **Archive** later. Archived plans keep their members' history.

![Screenshot: the Plans tab with Create plan](screenshot:sm-plans)

## How people join

1. Open **Settings**, then **Membership**, then **Membership admission**.
2. Choose the **Join mode**: open, invite-only or closed.
3. Choose whether staff approve, whether people pay first, the default plan and any joining fee.
4. Optionally switch on application questions and vouching under **Features**.

## Review requests

1. Open **Members**, then **Requests**.
2. Open a request and read the application answers and any vouches.
3. Press **Approve** or **Decline**.

**Invite member** invites someone straight in.

> **Who can do this:** anyone holding the members action reviews requests; plans and admission settings are the Space Manager's.
