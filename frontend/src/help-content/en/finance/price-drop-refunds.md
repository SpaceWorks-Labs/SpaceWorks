When a price goes down after people have paid for something not yet used, Space Works offers to give them the difference.

## Lower a price

1. Edit the price: a ticket type, a membership plan, a room, a machine type or a price list.
2. Save. If people have already paid, the **Lower this price?** dialog lists each one with the old price, the new price and the difference.
3. Press **Save and open refunds**, or **Keep editing** to think again.

![Screenshot: the Lower this price? dialog](screenshot:fin-price-drop)

Each payment gets its own refund case. A price rise never charges anyone more. A ticket type that has sold can only go down, and only if it's fixed-price.

## Approve the differences

1. Open **Refunds**. Price cases are labelled **Price lowered after payment**, **Prepaid job cancelled before it started** or **Job cost less than its prepaid quote**.
2. Read the explanation, then **Approve** or **Decline**.

Nothing is paid back until approved. A difference refund never ends a membership, and a declined difference isn't offered again.

> **Who can do this:** whoever edits the price opens the cases; the Space Manager or a finance administrator approves them.
