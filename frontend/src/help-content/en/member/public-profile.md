A public profile is a page about you at `/<your-handle>`. Nothing on it is public until you publish it.

## Publish your page

1. Open **Account**, then **Profile**.
2. Under **Public profile page**, choose a handle and press **Claim handle**.
3. Press **Publish…** and confirm.
4. Under **What visitors see**, switch on the sections you want. Every section starts hidden.
5. Press **View public page ↗** to check it. **Unpublish** takes it down.

![Screenshot: the Public profile page settings](screenshot:member-public-profile)

You can change your handle once every 30 days; the old link keeps redirecting for 30 days. Links you add must start with `https://`.

## Your maker profile

Under **Your maker profile**, add your projects and tick **Show my profile in this makerspace's member directory** to appear to other members.

> **Who can do this:** active members.
