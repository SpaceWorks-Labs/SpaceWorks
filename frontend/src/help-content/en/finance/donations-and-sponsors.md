## Donations

Open **Donations** in the menu. Its tabs are **Overview**, **Gifts**, **Campaigns**, **Pledges**, **Donors**, **Sponsors** and **Non-profit and receipts**.

1. Under **Campaigns**, press **New campaign**: a campaign with a goal and dates, or a fund that's always open. **Overview** shows each campaign's progress towards its goal.
2. Under **Gifts**, press **Record a gift** for money received in person. A gift someone promised online waits for payment: press **Mark received** when the money arrives, or **Confirm payment** when the giver says they have paid.
3. Press **Send receipt again** to email a giver their receipt. Press **Download CSV** to export the gift list.
4. To give money back, press **Return…** then **Open refund case**.

![Screenshot: the Donations Gifts tab](screenshot:fin-donations)

Every gift is income in the ledger. Returning one only opens a refund case.

## Non-profit profile and tax receipts

Under **Non-profit and receipts**, tick **This makerspace is a non-profit** and fill in:

- your **legal name** and **registration number** (trust, society, charity or company number);
- each **tax-exemption registration**: the scheme, its number and the dates it is valid. Schemes include India 80G and 12A/12AB, US 501(c)(3), and UK, Canadian and Australian charity numbers;
- who **signs the receipt** and their title, and a **receipt footer**.

What givers get:

- **Non-profit with a valid registration:** they can ask for a tax receipt. India 80G asks for their PAN and postal address. The receipt is fixed the day the gift arrives.
- **Not a non-profit, or no valid registration:** gifts still work. Every receipt says it is not a tax receipt.

India 12A on its own does not allow tax receipts; add your 80G registration too. A non-profit also gets a **Donate** link on its public pages.

## Donors and privacy

**Donors** lists who gave, how often and how much. A PAN or address never appears there or in the export. Every gift is anonymous on public pages unless the giver ticks **Name me among this campaign's supporters**.

## Sponsors

Open **Donations**, then **Sponsors**.

1. Under **Tiers and perks**, press **Add tier** with a suggested amount and its perks.
2. Under **Sponsors**, press **Add a sponsor**: name, tier, website, dates and a private contact.
3. Tick **Show on the public sponsor wall**, add a one-line description and a logo.
4. Under **Sponsorship money**, press **Record as owed**.
5. Link the sponsor to an event, and tick off perks as you deliver them.

To show the wall, add a **Sponsors** section to a public page. Contact details stay private.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager, or anyone given the **Fundraising** role. The non-profit profile is the Space Manager's alone. Area managers never see donation money.
