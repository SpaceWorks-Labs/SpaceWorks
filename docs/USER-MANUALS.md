# User manuals

Open **Help** in the application for guides matched to your role and the space's enabled features. The **Help** link beside a staff screen opens its related article. If a module is off, its workflows and help topics can be hidden. A custom role can expose several areas, each under its current permissions.

Suspension pauses ordinary service. Read-only mode can keep permitted reads available while blocking changes. Archive keeps the space's records and pauses its ordinary workflows. The platform superadmin manages these states through **Spaces**. A permission error or unavailable action should be checked against both your current role and the space's state.

Use the [switch-off reference](MODULES-OFF.md) to check what a disabled feature retains, and [logging and audit](logging-and-audit.md) to investigate a recorded action or failed request.
