## Open the till

1. Open the shop's front desk.
2. Where cash-ups are on, open a cash session with your opening float.

## Make a sale

1. Scan or search the products.
2. Look up the member if they're buying at a member price.
3. Take the payment (cash, UPI or card on your own terminal) and record how it was paid.
4. Print the receipt (80 mm or A4) or email it.

![Screenshot: the front-desk sale screen](screenshot:shop-pos)

Space Works records the payment; it doesn't talk to the card terminal.

## Hand over online orders

Find the order by its pickup code (orders show **Ready to collect** once marked ready), check the code with the buyer and press **Hand over**. The order then shows **Collected**.

## Cash up

At closing, enter the cash you counted. The difference from what's expected is shown, and a second person can confirm the count.

> **Who can do this:** the Shop Manager, the Space Manager, and staff given the shop selling action.
