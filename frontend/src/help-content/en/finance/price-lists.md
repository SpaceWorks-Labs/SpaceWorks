## Price lists

1. Open **Settings**, then **Pricing**.
2. Choose an area: **Lending**, **Machines**, **Bookings**, **Events** or **Membership**.
3. Choose **Free** or **Paid**, and set the rates, caps, grace periods and billing steps.
4. Check the **Worked example** to see what a typical member would pay, then save.

![Screenshot: a pricing area with its worked example](screenshot:fin-pricing)

With nothing configured, everything new is free. Individual items can inherit, be free, or have their own price.

## Member discounts

1. Under **Member discounts**, press **New discount**.
2. Name it, choose who gets it (all members, one plan's members or one member), the kind of discount and its dates.
3. Save.

Only the single best discount applies, then an event promo code on top.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager. Money settings can't be handed to anyone else.
