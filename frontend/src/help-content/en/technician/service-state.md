---
title: Take something out of service
summary: Mark a machine, item or room out of service while it's broken, and back in when it's fixed.
order: 30
keywords: out of service, back in service, outage, block bookings, unavailable, service state
features: machines.repairs, inventory.repairs, bookings.room_repairs
actions: manage_machines, edit_inventory, manage_bookings
---
1. Open the issue in **Repairs** and press **In or out of service**.
2. Choose **Out of service** or **Back in service**.
3. Give a reason and, if you know it, when it's expected back.
4. For a room, choose **Block new bookings** or **Only warn**. For items, say how many units.
5. Press **Save**.

![Screenshot: the In or out of service dialog](screenshot:tech-service-state)

When a room or machine goes out of service, bookings during the outage are listed and their bookers are emailed. Staff then cancel each one with a full refund, or keep it, from Bookings.

> **Who can do this:** Technicians within their coverage, and the area managers.
