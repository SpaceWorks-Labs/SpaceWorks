## Set up

1. Open the machine type, then **Settings**, then **Machine sessions**. Set the **Longest session** and press **Save**.
2. Open a machine's drawer, choose **Session QR** and **Show QR**. Print it and stick it on the machine.

## Watch and fix sessions

1. Open the **Sessions** view. It has three lists: **Live**, **To check** and **Closed**.
2. **Start for a member**: choose the member and press **Start session**.
3. On a session: **Stop session**, **Confirm**, **Correct time** (minutes and a reason), or **Assign member**.

![Screenshot: the Sessions view with Live and To check](screenshot:mm-sessions)

A session stops by itself at the longest length, when the member checks out, or at closing time, and then waits in **To check**. Sessions bill the type's hourly rate only while machine charges are switched on.

> **Who can do this:** Machine Managers and the Space Manager. Members need to be checked in and certified.
