## Organised events

1. Open the staff console and choose **Organized events**.
2. Switch between **All events**, **Upcoming** and **Past**.
3. Open an event to see it in the space that hosts it.

![Screenshot: the Organized events list](screenshot:org-organised-events)

Organisation members can register for an event when the hosting space opens it to participating organisations.

## Analytics

1. Choose **Org analytics**.
2. **Choose an organization** and a **Report**.
3. Read the **Organization total** and the **Breakdown by makerspace**.

Different currencies and units are never added together; each shows on its own line.

> **Who can do this:** organisation members and governors. These tabs are hidden on a site that runs only one space.
