# Hosting other makerspaces

This runbook has moved. Space Works has one deployment mode (self-hosted); running spaces for other
people is an optional switch on a normal install. The full operator guide, covering wildcard DNS and TLS,
the superadmin workflow, plans and limits, platform fallbacks, backups, support and upgrades, is the
section **Plans and hosting other makerspaces (optional)** in [Self-hosting](self-hosting.md#plans-and-hosting-other-makerspaces-optional).
