Some spaces sell kits, consumables and merchandise. You order online or at the front desk, and collect in person; nothing is shipped.

## Order online

1. Open the space's **Shop**.
2. Browse. Each product shows **In stock**, **Low** or **Sold out**.
3. Choose a size or variant and add it to your order.
4. Review the total. If a price changed since you added it, you see the new total first.
5. Pay online, or choose to pay at the desk.

![Screenshot: the shop with products and an order summary](screenshot:member-shop)

Where the space holds stock for orders, your items are set aside while you pay: by default 15 minutes for online payment and 48 hours for a pay-at-desk order. The space can change these times.

## Collect

1. Wait for your order to show **Ready to collect**.
2. Collect it at the space, or at the event you chose.
3. Show your pickup code at the desk.

If you don't collect a paid order, the space reminds you (by default after 3 and 7 days). After the space's collection window (30 days by default) staff may cancel it, and a staff-approved refund case is opened for the money.

## Returns

Bring an item back within the space's returns window. Staff check it and any refund goes through a staff-approved refund case.

> **Who can do this:** members, and guests who confirm their email with a code where the space allows guest checkout. Members may see member prices.
