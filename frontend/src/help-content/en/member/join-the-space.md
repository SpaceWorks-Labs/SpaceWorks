Spaces that run memberships ask you to join before you borrow, book or use machines. How you join depends on the space.

## Join

1. Sign in and open **Home** in your member area. Find **Join this makerspace**.
2. If the space offers plans, pick one under **Choose a plan**.
3. Answer any application questions.
4. Press the button the space offers: **Join instantly**, **Apply to join** (pay first) or **Request to join** (staff approval or vouching).
5. Follow the status card. It reads **Membership request sent**, **Membership awaiting payment** or **Membership awaiting vouches**.
6. Accept the waiver with **Accept waiver** when it appears.

![Screenshot: the Join this makerspace card on the member Home tab](screenshot:member-join)

**Cancel application** withdraws a request. **Download application data** saves a copy of your answers.

## When someone needs to vouch for you

Some spaces ask existing members to vouch for newcomers.

1. Share your application number with a member who knows you.
2. They enter it under **Vouch for an applicant** and press **Record my vouch**.

By default one vouch is enough. Applications expire after 30 days. A vouch is permanent and carries the voucher's name.

## Invitations

- No invitation yet? Use **Ask for an invitation** on Home, fill in your details and press **Send request**.
- Invited by email? Verify your email first, then claim it under **Pending invitations**.
- Members who may refer others find **Invite a friend** under **Account**.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with an account. Closed spaces show "Membership applications are closed"; invite-only spaces need an invitation link.
