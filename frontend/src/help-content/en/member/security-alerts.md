Space Works watches for sign-ins that look unusual and tells you about important changes to your account.

## "We don't recognise this device or network"

When you sign in with a password from a new device or network, or after several failed attempts, we may ask for one more check.

1. Sign in with your password as usual.
2. Open the email with your six-digit code.
3. Type it into **Emailed code** and press **Verify**.
4. Nothing arrived? Press **Send a new code**. You can ask twice.

If you already use an authenticator or a passkey, you confirm with that instead of an email.

## Security emails

We email you after a new sign-in, a password change, a change to your two-step sign-in, and when your account is locked. Each email has a link for when it wasn't you.

## If it wasn't you

1. Open the link in the email. The page reads **Didn't do this?**
2. Press **Secure my account**. If it was you after all, press **It was me**.
3. On **Your account is secured**, press **Choose a new password** and use the code we email.

![Screenshot: the Didn't do this? page](screenshot:member-not-me)

Securing the account signs you out everywhere, forgets your devices and turns off your old password. Your passkeys and authenticator stay. The link works once and expires after a few days.

> **Who can do this:** every account holder. If you can no longer use your authenticator or passkey, ask a Space Manager or the platform administrator to reset your two-step sign-in.
