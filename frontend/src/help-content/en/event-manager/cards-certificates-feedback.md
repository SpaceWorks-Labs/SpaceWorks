## Feedback

1. Open **More**, then **Feedback**. Choose a **Feedback template** and press **Apply template**.
2. Set the survey title and thank-you message.
3. Optionally require attendance and issue an attendance certificate with each answer.
4. Press **Save survey**, and later **Open responses**.

Questions that already have answers can't be edited.

## Certificates

Under **More**, open the section whose name includes **Certificates**. Its label reflects the enabled tools: **Certificates** on its own, or a combined label such as **Certificates, cards and tickets**. Design the certificate, press **Preview exact PDF**, then save it with **Save this kind's design** (or **Save the default design**). Each save creates a new revision; certificates already issued keep the design they were issued with.

To issue in bulk, open **Issue certificates** and tick **Who should receive one?**: **Attendance**, **Participants**, **Winners and finalists** or **Thank-yous**. Press **Preview recipients**, check the list and any skipped people, tick the confirmation box and press **Issue certificates**. Issued certificates can be downloaded or withdrawn.

## Signatories and verification

In the certificate designer, open **Signatories**. Save each signatory's name, title, account and signature as appropriate. If **Signatories must approve the design** is enabled, ask each person to open **Certificate designs to approve** in their member area, choose **Review design**, and press **Approve** after checking the PDF.

Issuance and delivery wait for the required design approvals. Existing approvals apply to the design revision they name.

The public verification page shows **File fingerprint**. Under **Check a certificate file**, choose or drop the issued PDF to compare it on your device. Holder privacy controls determine which personal details that page displays.

## Event cards and tickets

Under **More**, the section is **Cards and tickets** when both tools are on without certificates, **ID cards** for cards only, or **Ticket design** for tickets only. If certificates are also on, the label combines the available tools.

Event ID cards enable **ID cards** and **Card design**. Ticket designs independently enable **Ticket design**. A disabled tool is hidden without hiding the other enabled tools; each still needs the existing permission to edit the event.

1. Design the card and publish it.
2. In **ID cards**, press **Issue cards now**, and set each person's role: attendee, speaker, mentor, judge, volunteer or crew.
3. Use **All cards (PDF)** or **All cards (ZIP)** to print, or **Revoke** one.
4. In **Ticket design**, design the PDF ticket attached to each confirmation email.

![Screenshot: Cards and tickets in the event drawer](screenshot:em-event-cards)

Once a card design is published, confirmed registrants get their card automatically. The designer works like the member card designer; see [Member cards and card designs](help:space-manager/member-cards).

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers, the Space Manager, and anyone who can edit the event.
