These extras sit on top of [Hackathons and judging](help:event-manager/hackathons-and-judging). Each has its own switch.

## Finalists

1. Finalize a round.
2. Advance finalists: the top so many, or hand-picked. Preview, then apply.
3. Publish the finalists when you're ready; that's a separate step.

Disqualified or withdrawn projects never advance. Results come from the final round. Judges move through their list with **Score next project**, and a progress view shows projects below target and unusual scores.

## Prize payouts

Set a prize per winner, then record each payout by hand when it's paid. A mistake is corrected with a reversal entry, never by editing. Payouts are expenses in the finance ledger; nothing is paid automatically.

## Entry fees and paid teams

- With an entry fee, a project stays a draft until its fee is paid.
- With paid teams, the team leader pays once, at one fixed price per team, whatever its size.

A disqualification or rejection opens a refund case for staff to decide.

## Emails, views and the gallery

- Results and submission-receipt emails are per-event switches, off for new events.
- Save a filtered list of guests, teams or submissions as a personal or shared view, and export exactly what you see to CSV. A saved view never grants access.
- The public gallery can be searched and sorted; it never shows winners before results are published.
