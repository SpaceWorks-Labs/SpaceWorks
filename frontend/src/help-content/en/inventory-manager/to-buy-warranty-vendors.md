## The To-Buy list

1. Open **To Buy** and press **Add item**: name, quantity, estimated cost and a link.
2. Move it along with **Approve**, **Mark ordered** and **Mark received**, or simply **Mark bought** with the actual cost and the receipt.
3. Press **Move to inventory** to put it on the shelf.

![Screenshot: the To Buy list with item statuses](screenshot:im-to-buy)

Items marked **Auto · low stock** were drafted automatically when a consumable or filament ran low. What you spend is recorded in the finance ledger.

## Warranties

Add a warranty from an item's unit or a machine: purchase date, expiry and the vendor. **Warranties** lists them all; filter by what expires soon or what's missing documents.

## Vendors

Where **Vendors** is switched on, open the **Vendors** view and press **Add vendor** to save a supplier for To-Buy items and warranties. **Archive** retires one.

> **Who can do this:** Inventory Managers, Machine Managers and the Space Manager, each for their own items and machines.
