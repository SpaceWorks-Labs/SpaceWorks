## Give a helper access to one resource

1. Open the space, then **Settings**, then **Staff access**.
2. Enter their email and tick what they may do: edit details, manage reservations, check in, or publish the page.
3. Press **Invite staff**. The link is shown once; tick **Email the invitation link** to send it too.

![Screenshot: the Staff access invite form](screenshot:bm-staff-access)

The helper accepts, then works from **Assigned resources**. Invitations expire after seven days, and grants never include pricing, rules or money.

## A web page for the resource

1. Open the space, then **Website**. **Add section** and **Save content**.
2. Check **Private preview**, then **Release website**.
3. To show it on another site, copy the snippet from **Sharing**, then **Embed card**. The Space Manager must allow that site's address first.

## Email upcoming bookers

1. Open the space, then **Broadcasts**.
2. Write a subject and message, choose which bookings (by date and status) and press **Save draft**.
3. Press **Send broadcast**. Sent emails can't be recalled.

> **Who can do this:** Booking Managers and the Space Manager; helpers only for what they were granted.
