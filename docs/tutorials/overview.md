# Tutorials

Each tutorial follows one outcome from start to finish. Every step says what to do and what you should see when it worked. Screens are described in words, so you can follow along against your own install.

> **Tip** Do the tutorials in order the first time. Later ones assume the areas and staff you set up in earlier ones. After that, jump to whichever outcome you need.

| # | Tutorial | You finish with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Set up your first makerspace](first-makerspace.md) | A running install, a configured space, a first staff member and a public page |
| 2 | [Lend your first tool](lend-your-first-tool.md) | An item, a labelled box, and one loan from request to return, with evidence |
| 3 | [Run your first event](first-event.md) | A published event with tickets, registrations, a guest pass and check-in |
| 4 | [Take room bookings](room-bookings.md) | A bookable room, plan-included hours, and an organization booking drawing from an hours bank |
| 5 | [Take payments](take-payments.md) | Charges tracked, an offline receipt recorded, an online provider connected, a refund decided |
| 6 | [Machines and inductions](machines-and-inductions.md) | A machine type, a certification, a session and a maintenance schedule |
| 7 | [Connect analytics](connect-analytics.md) | Metabase or Grafana dashboards for one space inside the staff console |

## Before you start

- You need a running Space Works install and a Space Manager account. Tutorial 1 covers both.
- Menu names in these pages match the staff console. A name in **bold** is a button, tab or menu entry you can search for on screen. If a menu entry is missing, your space probably has that area switched off under **Settings**, **Areas & add-ons**, or your role does not include it. See [Choose what your space runs](../choosing-modules.md).
- Everything you switch off keeps its data. You cannot lose records by experimenting with switches.

## Where next

When you are done, the [User guides](../user-guides/overview.md) describe each role's daily work, and the in-app **Help** (`/help`) has a short article for every screen.
