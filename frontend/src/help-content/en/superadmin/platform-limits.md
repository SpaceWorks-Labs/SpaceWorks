Only a platform administrator sees **Limits & hosting**. Space managers cannot change a space's limits.

1. Open **Limits & hosting** in the platform console.
2. Find the space in the list. Its bar shows its highest use against its limits.
3. Press **Edit limits** on that space.
4. Set the numbers you want and save. Leave a field empty for no limit.

**Every limit defaults to none.** A new space can use as much as it needs until you set a cap, so nothing is blocked by surprise.

Spaces near or at a limit show **Near limit** or **At limit**. Use the bulk apply section to set one limit on many spaces at once.
