All of this lives under **More**, then **Tickets & invites**.

## Ticket types

1. Under **Ticket types**, fill in the name, price, audience and capacity (0 means unlimited), and when sales start and end.
2. Press **Add ticket type**.

A ticket's price and audience lock once someone registers with it. A used ticket can't be deleted: **Deactivate** it instead.

## Promo codes

1. Under **Promo codes**, choose a percentage or fixed discount, optionally your own code, how many uses and when it expires.
2. Press **Create promo code**. Copy it: it's shown once.

![Screenshot: Tickets & invites with ticket types and promo codes](screenshot:em-tickets)

## Invitations

1. Under **Invitations**, optionally add one email, set the number of uses and the expiry, and press **Create invitation**.
2. For many people, use **Bulk email invitations** (up to 200 addresses).

Each code is shown once.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers and the Space Manager; helpers granted invitation management handle invitations.
