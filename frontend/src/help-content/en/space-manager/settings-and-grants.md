**Settings** is the hub for everything about your space. Use **Search settings** at the top to jump straight to one ("loan days", "logo", "WhatsApp").

## Give an area's settings to its manager

By default, area managers see their area's settings but can't change them.

1. Open **Settings**. Under **Start here**, find **Who can change settings**.
2. Pick a row: **Event settings**, **Booking settings**, **Machine settings** or **Lending settings**.
3. Press **Give control to**, choose a person and press **Give control**.
4. Press **Remove** to take it back.

![Screenshot: the Who can change settings card](screenshot:sm-settings-grants)

Control is given person by person, never to a whole role.

## Lock a setting

1. Open the setting.
2. Press **Lock for managers**. It shows **Locked for managers**, and others see it's locked by the Space Manager.

A lock never applies to you.

## Exceptions

- Inventory Managers can always change loan length and renewals, without a grant (unless you lock them).
- Money settings (tax, billing, payments and charges) and security settings are yours alone and can't be handed over.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager gives and removes control. Everything is recorded in the audit log.
