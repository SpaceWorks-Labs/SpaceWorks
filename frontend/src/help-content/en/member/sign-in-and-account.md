One Space Works account works at every space you join on this site.

## Create an account

1. Open your member area (`/member`) and choose **Sign in to your account**.
2. Switch to **Create account**.
3. Fill in your name, email and a password. A phone number is optional.
4. Press **Create account**, then open the email we send and verify your address.
5. Sign in. If you are asked to **Verify your email**, type the code from the email and press **Verify email**.

Some spaces also offer Google, Apple or GitHub buttons. If self sign-up is closed, the screen asks you to ask a staff member to add you.

## Forgot your password

1. Go to `/reset-password`.
2. Enter your email and press **Request recovery code**.
3. Type the six-digit code from the email and your new password twice.
4. Press **Update password**, then **Go to sign in**.

The new password needs at least 8 characters. If the space has no email set up, password recovery is unavailable; ask the staff.

## Your account page

Open **Account** in your member area. Depending on what the space offers you will see **Profile**, **Security**, **Membership**, **History**, **Privacy**, **Your data** and **Delete account**.

- **Profile**: your display name and contact phone. These never change the email you sign in with.
- **Security**: two-step sign-in and your signed-in sessions. Use **Revoke session** on a device you no longer use.

## Delete your account, or bring it back

For tools that act as you, use [Personal API tokens and connected apps](help:member/api-access-and-apps).

1. Open **Account** and choose **Delete account**.
2. Settle anything listed as blocking, such as money you owe.
3. Type `DELETE` and press **Delete account permanently**.

Deleting covers every space you belong to. For a while afterwards (90 days unless the site says otherwise) you can restore it: request a password recovery, follow the restore link within 24 hours and set a new password on **Restore your account**. Memberships and sessions do not come back.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with an account. Staff accounts always keep password sign-in.
