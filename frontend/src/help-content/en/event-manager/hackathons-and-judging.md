A hackathon adds **Programme setup**, **Applications**, **Teams and projects** and **Judging** under the event's **More**.

## Admissions

Review applications and release offers in batches; see [Applications and review stages](help:event-manager/applications-and-review).

## Judging

1. Under **Judging**, set up a round and press **Open and freeze rubric**.
2. **Assign judges**. Judges score the projects assigned to them.
3. **Close scoring**, then **Finalize round**.
4. **Announce results**, and **Publish gallery selection** for the public gallery.

![Screenshot: the Judging round controls](screenshot:em-judging)

Where pairwise judging is on, judges instead say which of two projects was better; each project needs at least three comparisons. The first-commit check on code is information only and never disqualifies anyone.

## Help desks

- **Mentor Desk**: the help queue, assignments, check-in rounds and conflicts.
- **Volunteers**: shift sign-ups and corrected minutes, with a reason.
- **Travel support**: claims against a reimbursement pool, with payouts recorded by finance.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers, the Space Manager, and helpers granted hackathon management, review, teams, judging, mentoring or volunteers.
