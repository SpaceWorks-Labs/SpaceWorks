Many spaces ask you to check in when you arrive. Borrowing, machine jobs and sessions may need a current check-in.

## Check in

1. Open **Home** in your member area and find **Presence**.
2. Press **Check in** and allow your browser to use your location once.
3. Answer **How long are you staying?**
4. Answer **What are you here for?**: working on a project, visiting, or attending an event.
5. Tick **Hide me on the display** if you'd rather not appear on the space's "People here now" screen.
6. Press **Check in**.

![Screenshot: the Presence panel with the Check in button](screenshot:member-check-in)

## While you're there

Use **Extend from now** to stay longer, **Notify me before it ends** for a reminder, or **End check-in** when you leave.

Your location is used once to confirm you're at the space and is never stored. If you're outside the area, the check-in is refused; ask staff to check you in.

> **Who can do this:** signed-in members and guests of spaces that run presence.
