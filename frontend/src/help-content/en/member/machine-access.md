A space manager gives you access to the machines you may use. You do not need a certificate or a training record.

## Check your access

1. Open **Profile & card** in your member area.
2. Choose **My machine access**.
3. Each line shows the machine, or every machine of a type, the day access was given, and when it ends. **Has access** means you can use it now; **Ended** means ask a space manager to renew it.

![Screenshot: My machine access with one active and one ended line](screenshot:member-machine-access)

Machines listed under **Open to all members** need no access from anyone.

If a scan, a job request or a booking says you have no access to a machine, ask a space manager to give it to you. You will get a message when access is given, removed, or about to end.

> **Who can do this:** active members. Space managers and machine managers give access; see [Machine access](help:machine-manager/machine-access).
