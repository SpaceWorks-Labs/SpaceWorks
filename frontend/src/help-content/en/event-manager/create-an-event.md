## Create

1. Open **Events** and press **New event**.
2. Fill in the form; a preview shows how it will look. Press **Create event**. It starts as a draft.
3. Open **More**, then **Settings**, then **Publishing**. Answer the three questions: who can see it, who can find it, and how people attend.
4. Press **Publish**.

![Screenshot: the New event form with its preview](screenshot:em-new-event)

To give the listing and event page a picture, open **More**, then **Settings**, and under **Event photo** upload a **Public event image** (a wide image works best). Upload again to replace it, or use **Remove**. A series has its own image in its drawer.

Later, use **Complete event** after it's over, or **Cancel event**: cancelling tells everyone registered and closes registration.

## The roster

Open **Registrations** to see who's going. Counts on **Overview** show going, waitlist, awaiting approval and checked in. Row actions: **Approve**, **Reject**, **Promote** from the waitlist, **Mark attended**, **Correct attendance**, **Cancel registration**. **Add registration** registers someone yourself.

Contact details and answers are for staff only.

## A series

1. In **Events**, open **Series** and press **New series**.
2. Set the first date, how it repeats, the weekdays and prices. Press **Create series**, then **Publish series**.
3. **Edit future occurrences** changes the dates still to come. **Extend now**, **Complete series** or **Cancel series** as needed.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers and the Space Manager; helpers only for the parts they were granted.


## Event figures on the public stats page

Open **Events → Event settings → What appears in public stats** to choose upcoming-event cards, upcoming counts, events held and checked-in attendance. Save the changed choices with **Save public stats**. Your event-settings grant and the Space Manager's locks decide which switches you can change. Counts below three remain suppressed; hiding a figure keeps its source records and also hides it on the TV board. Overall page settings remain with the Space Manager.
