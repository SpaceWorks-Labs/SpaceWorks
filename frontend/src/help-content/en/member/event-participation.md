## "Are you still coming?"

Organisers sometimes ask registered people to confirm before the day.

1. Open the link in the email, or go to the event in your **Events** tab.
2. Press **Yes, I'm coming** or **No, I can't come**.

Saying no frees your place for someone else. For a paid place, any refund is decided by the organisers under the event's policy; it is never automatic.

## Join the crew

1. Open the crew sign-up link the organisers shared. The page is **Join the crew**.
2. Choose what you'd like to help with and send it.
3. If the organisers review sign-ups, wait for their approval.

Crew places are free and never take an attendee's place. Press **Withdraw** at any time.

## Review applications

When you're on a review panel, your workspace is **Application reviews** (`/member/application-reviews`).

1. Open it and pick an application assigned to you.
2. Score each criterion and add your notes.
3. Press **Submit review**, or **I have a conflict** if you know the applicant.

You see only your own stage and the applications assigned to you. Some panels are blind: names and identifying answers are hidden.

> **Who can do this:** registered attendees answer attendance requests; any signed-in person may sign up to a crew; panel reviewers are chosen by the organisers.
