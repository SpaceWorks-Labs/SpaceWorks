## Create a resource

1. Open **Bookings**, then **Spaces**, and press **New space**.
2. Fill in its name, type, capacity, location and description. For machine time, choose **Machine time** and pick the machine: its type's training rules apply automatically.
3. Set the booking price, whether it's listed publicly, and when it's available.
4. Press **Create space**.

![Screenshot: the New space form](screenshot:bm-new-space)

Each space has **Requests**, **Calendar**, **Issues**, **Photos** and **Settings**. In Settings, the **Booking listing photo** is the picture shown on the public booking page; upload again to replace it or use **Remove**. In Settings, **Clone resource** copies it and **Deactivate space** hides it and stops new bookings.

## Book for a member

1. Open the space and press **New booking**, then **Book for a member**.
2. Choose the member, the start and the end.
3. To repeat it weekly, tick the repeat option and set how many times (2 to 52).
4. Add a staff note, and a certification override reason if needed. Press **Create booking**.

The member needs an active membership and a current waiver. Each repeat is checked on its own.

Booking rules for the whole space (cut-offs, limits) belong to the Space Manager.

> **Who can do this:** Booking Managers and the Space Manager.


## Booking count on public stats

Open **Bookings → Booking settings → What appears in public stats** to choose whether the booking count appears publicly. Press **Save public stats** after changing it. The Space Manager must grant booking-settings control and may lock this choice. The setting also applies to the TV board; counts below three remain suppressed. It does not publish booking identities or delete bookings. Overall stats-page settings remain with the Space Manager.
