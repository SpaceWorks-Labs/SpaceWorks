**Finance** shows the money side of your space: **Income and spending**, **Expenses and receipts** and **Event money**.

## Record an expense

1. Open **Finance** and press **Record money**, then **Record an expense**.
2. Fill in the amount, method, date and reference, and attach the receipt photo or PDF.
3. Save.

![Screenshot: the Record an expense form](screenshot:fin-expense)

**Record other income** works the same way for money that isn't a member charge. **Void entry** cancels an entry with a reason; nothing is ever deleted.

## Where the numbers come from

Every charge paid, every expense, To-Buy purchase, prize payout and refund lands here, broken down by month, area and machine type. Spending is always recorded, even when the ledger screens are switched off.

## Who sees what

Each area manager sees their own area's money: an Event Manager sees events money, a Machine Manager machine money, and so on. Finance staff and the Space Manager see everything.

> **Who can do this:** finance staff and the Space Manager, and area managers for their own area.
