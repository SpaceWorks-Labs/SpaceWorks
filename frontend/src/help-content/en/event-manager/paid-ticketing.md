With ticket orders on, a buyer can buy several places and extras at once. Set each up under **More**, then **Tickets & invites**.

## Order options for a ticket

1. Create a ticket type first.
2. Open **Ticket order options** for it.
3. Under **Pricing**, choose **Fixed price** or **Pay what you want** (with the lowest amount).
4. Set the people per ticket, the fewest and most per order, and its tier group and order.
5. Tick **Hidden: sold only with an access code** for a secret ticket. Press **Save options**.

Paid orders appear under **Ticket orders**. Promo codes work only on fixed-price tickets.

## Add-ons

Under **Add-ons**, give each extra a name, kind (kit, meal, merchandise, session seat, other), price, sizes and stock, then **Add add-on**. **Stop selling** pauses one. Add-on stock belongs to the event, never to the workshop inventory.

## Sales rules

Under **Sales rules**, allow attendees to pass their ticket to someone else, or to change ticket type. Press **Save sales rules**.

## Series passes

Open the series, then **Passes**. Name the pass, set its price, how many are available and how much is owed back for each cancelled date. A cancelled date opens a refund case for staff to decide.

## Taking payment at the desk

On an unpaid row in **Registrations**, open **Collect payment**, choose how it was received and add a reference, then **Record payment**. Or **Email pay link**. You record money actually received; nothing is taken automatically.

## Refund policy

Under **Refund policy**, tick to use this event's own policy and set it. After the first sale it can only become more generous. Refunds stay staff-approved.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers and the Space Manager; desk payments by staff with check-in access.
