## Enrol a bridge

1. Open **Machines**, choose **Views**, then **Device adapters**.
2. Press **Enrol a bridge**, name it after where it lives, then press **Enrol bridge**.
3. Copy the one-time token into the bridge, then press **I've copied it**.

![Screenshot: the Device adapters list](screenshot:mm-adapters)

The token is shown once and cannot be read again. If your plan limits how many bridges you can enrol, Space Works says so and asks you to revoke one you no longer use.

## Bind what it looks after

Open a bridge and choose **Bindings**. Pick a machine, machine type, storage location, box, item, door or space, tick what the adapter does for it, and press **Bind**. Storage locations are staff only and never appear on public pages. **Status and controls** then shows each target's state, progress, temperatures and errors, with **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel job** and **Power**. Each one asks first and is recorded in the audit log with your name.

## Shelf pointer

For a bridge with a pointer, the **Shelf pointer** tab can **Indicate** one place, **Walk the shelves** one location at a time, or **Calibrate**: jog the pointer, then save its position for a location. Locations with no saved position are skipped until you calibrate them.

## Credentials, camera and logs

With the access switch on, **Credentials** lists members' NFC tags and PINs. Only a one-way digest is kept, so a PIN is never shown again. With the camera switch on, **Watch** opens a five-minute watch; the camera sends nothing until you start one. **Logs** lists commands, events, access decisions and usage. **Revoke adapter** cuts a bridge off for good.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager, or anyone given the devices action. Camera watch and credentials also need their own switches on.
