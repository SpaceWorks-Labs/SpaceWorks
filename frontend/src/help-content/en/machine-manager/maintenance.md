## Schedule it

1. Open a machine's drawer, then **Maintenance**, and press **New schedule**.
2. Choose the trigger: every so many days, or (where usage meters are on) every so many hours or jobs.
3. Choose whether the next date counts from when it was done, or follows a fixed calendar.
4. Press **Create schedule**.

## Log it

Press **Log maintenance** (or **Complete maintenance** on a due item), say what was done and add parts and notes.

![Screenshot: a machine's Maintenance tab with schedules](screenshot:mm-maintenance)

Overdue maintenance locks the machine: it shows **Locked out** and jobs can't start on it until it's done.

## Work orders

For a one-off job: **Work orders**, **New work order** (what needs doing, priority, due date), **Start work**, then **Close work order** with what was done, the cost and parts.

## Reminders and calendar

- Set the type's **Reminder policy** to warn maintainers before and after a due date.
- **Maintenance calendar**, then **Create or rotate calendar link**, gives you a personal calendar feed.

> **Who can do this:** Machine Managers and the Space Manager; Technicians run scheduled maintenance from Repairs for what they cover.
