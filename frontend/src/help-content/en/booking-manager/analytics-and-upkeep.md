## Analytics and exports

1. Open the space, then **Analytics**.
2. Choose a start and end date.
3. Download **Utilization CSV**, **Reservation CSV**, **Custom answers CSV** or **Utilization PDF**.

Arrival counts show who was scanned in, not how the room was actually used.

## Room upkeep

- **Issues** in Bookings lists problems members flagged; **Resolve** then **Mark resolved**.
- Where room maintenance is on, add a **Room schedule** under **Repairs**, **Maintenance**: the room, what to do, how often and the first due date.
- Where room repairs are on, raise a **New repair job** for the room. See [Manage repairs and technicians](help:technician/managing-repairs).
- To close a room for repairs, use **In or out of service** and choose **Block new bookings** or **Only warn**.

> **Who can do this:** Booking Managers and the Space Manager. Analytics also needs the reports module and audit access.
