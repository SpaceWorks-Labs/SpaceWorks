## Download your data

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data**.
2. Press **Request data export**.
3. When it shows as available, press **Prepare download link**, then **Download ZIP**.

You can ask once a day. The download holds your records as JSON; photos and uploaded files are left out, and the link expires.

## Consents

Open **Account**, then **Privacy**. Under **Consents**, tick or untick each purpose. Your choice applies at once.

## Ask a space to erase your data

1. Open **Account**, then **Privacy**.
2. Under **Erase my data here**, add a note if you like.
3. Press **Ask to erase my data**. **Withdraw** cancels a pending request.

![Screenshot: the Privacy section with Consents and Erase my data here](screenshot:member-privacy)

If staff approve, your membership at that space ends and your record, profile and answers there are erased. Payment records and the audit trail stay, as the law requires, and your account itself stays. To remove your whole account, see [Sign in, create an account and manage it](help:member/sign-in-and-account).

> **Who can do this:** active members of spaces that switch these on.
