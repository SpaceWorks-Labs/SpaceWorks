An ID programme issues cards for an organisation linked to your space: a college's student IDs, for example.

## Create one

1. Open **Member cards**, then **ID programmes**.
2. Pick the **Organisation**. Add it under Organisations first if it isn't there.
3. Name the programme, choose what to **Start from** (blank, student, volunteer or staff ID) and press **Create**.
4. In **Settings**, set **Status** to Open, a number prefix of 2 to 6 letters, how long cards are valid, whether a photo is needed, and the join questions. Press **Save settings**.
5. Copy the **Join link** and share it.
6. Design the card in **Card design**.

![Screenshot: an ID programme's Settings tab](screenshot:sm-id-programme)

Card numbers look like `PREFIX-YEAR-0001`. Joining needs the programme open and joining switched on.

## Approve people

1. Open **Members** in the programme. Filter by **Waiting**, **Approved**, **Not approved** or **Revoked**.
2. Select people and press **Approve**, **Reject…**, **Revoke…** or **Renew**. Rejecting and revoking need a staff-only reason.
3. **Add and invite** adds one person by name and email.

## Import a list

Under **Import**, choose a CSV file (or a Google Sheets link where switched on), match the name and email columns, check the preview and press **Add**. Everyone gets an emailed invitation; nothing is linked until they accept.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager and anyone given the ID programme action. Holder details stay masked for people who can only view programmes.
