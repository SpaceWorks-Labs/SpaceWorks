Containers are the boxes, bins and shelves your items live in. Each has a QR label, and a handover always scans the box.

## Add a container

1. Open **Containers** and press **New container**.
2. Choose whether it's a box, a bin or a shelf, and name it.
3. Save, then print its label from [QR labels](help:inventory-manager/qr-tools).

![Screenshot: the Containers list](screenshot:im-containers)

Search the list, **Edit** a container, or open its **Scan history** to see every time it was scanned.

## Kits

Where **Kit checklists** is switched on, a kit lists its parts. At handover and at return, staff count each part; missing parts stay on the borrower's record.

> **Who can do this:** staff with the QR action (Inventory Manager, Space Manager).
