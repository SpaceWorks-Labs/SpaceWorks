Organisers can give you access to a single event or a single bookable resource without a staff role.

1. Open the invitation email and its link. Sign in with the address it was sent to.
2. Press **Accept staff invitation**.
3. In the staff console, open **Assigned resources**. Each event or resource you help with is listed with what you may do.
4. Open one and work as usual: only the parts you were granted appear.

![Screenshot: the Assigned resources list](screenshot:em-assigned-resources)

Invitations expire after seven days. What you can do (check people in, manage registrations, judge, and so on) is set by the organiser and can change at any time.

> **Who can do this:** anyone invited to help with one event or resource.
