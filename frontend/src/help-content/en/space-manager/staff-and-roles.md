Space Works has built-in roles. There are no separate staff accounts: a staff role is granted on a person's one account, for one space. A person can hold several roles at once; together they decide what that person sees.

- **Inventory Manager**: requests, handovers, items, QR codes and stocktakes.
- **Machine Manager**: machines, jobs, sessions and maintenance, for the machine types you pick.
- **Event Manager**: events, tickets, check-in and programmes.
- **Booking Manager**: rooms and resources, and booking requests.
- **Handover**: the front desk, handing tools over and taking them back.
- **Technician**: repair jobs assigned to them, scheduled maintenance and service state.
- **Shop Manager**: the shop, where the space runs one.

Space Manager and Member are each held alone. Only the platform administrator gives or removes the Space Manager role.

## Add someone

1. Open **Users** (shown as **Staff & roles**) and press **Add staff**.
2. Under **Add by**, choose **A member of this space** and find them, or **Invite by email**.
3. Tick their roles. For a Machine Manager or Technician, choose what they cover.
4. Press **Grant roles** or **Send invitation**.

## Change or remove access

On a person's row: **Change roles** then **Save roles**, **Revoke**, **Restore**, or **Reset authenticator** if they've lost their two-step device. Revoking ends that space's staff access straight away but keeps their account, their membership and everything they did; they can still borrow and book as a member. Restoring brings the roles back. If they had a staff ID card it ends with the role, and it is reissued with the same number when their roles change.

An email invitation goes only to the address you typed, carries the roles and machine choices you picked, and is accepted while signed in with a fresh emailed code. Nobody can change their own roles, and the last Space Manager cannot be removed.

**Staff ID cards**, when your space has them switched on, are issued from the **Member cards** screen.

The **Invitations** tab lists pending invites (**Resend**, **Revoke**); the **Roles** tab shows what each role holds.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager. You can only give roles whose actions you hold yourself. A Machine Manager with no machine types picked sees no machines.
