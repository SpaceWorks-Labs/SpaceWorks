Damaged units land on the **To-be-fixed** shelf (the **Repair shelf** in the menu). They come from **Reject as broken** at handover, damaged returns, or **Move to repair shelf** on an item.

## Clear the shelf

1. Open **To-be-fixed**.
2. For a unit that's mended, set the quantity and press **Repaired**. It's back in stock.
3. For one that can't be saved, use **Scrap** from its menu and confirm.

![Screenshot: the To be fixed shelf](screenshot:im-to-be-fixed)

Scrapped units leave the inventory for good. Where the space runs repair jobs, a bigger fix can go to a technician; see [Repair jobs](help:technician/your-jobs).

> **Who can do this:** Inventory Managers and the Space Manager.
