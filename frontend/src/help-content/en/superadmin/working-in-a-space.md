## Choose a space

1. After signing in, use **Find a makerspace** and pick one under **Your makerspaces**.
2. A banner says you're working in that space as the platform superadmin, and that every change is audited there.
3. Use any screen as the space's Space Manager would.
4. Use **Switch makerspace** to move to another.

![Screenshot: the superadmin banner inside a space](screenshot:sa-in-space)

## Personal data is masked

Names, emails and phone numbers show masked for you until you open a window.

1. On the banner, press **Open PII access**.
2. Type a reason (10 to 300 characters).
3. Press **Open for 30 minutes**.
4. Press **Close now** when you're done.

The space's managers can see every window and its reason under **Recent platform access**. Exports of personal data are refused without an open window.

> **Who can do this:** the platform superadmin.
