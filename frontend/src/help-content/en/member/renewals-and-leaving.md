Your **Home** tab shows your **Membership plan** and the date it is current until.

## Renew

1. Before the end date, a renewal charge appears in **Payments**.
2. Pay it online with **Pay now**, or pay at the space. See [Pay what you owe](help:member/pay-what-you-owe).

Spaces usually remind you 14, 7 and 1 days before your membership ends. If a space pauses lapsed members, Home says borrowing is paused until you renew.

## Stop renewing

1. Open **Account**, then **Membership**.
2. Press **Cancel renewal**, give a reason and press **Confirm cancellation**.

Your membership stays active until its end date.

## Leave now

1. Open **Account**, then **Membership**.
2. Press **Leave makerspace**, then **Confirm leave**.

Leaving ends your membership at once. A person who is the space's only manager can't leave until someone else takes over.

> **Who can do this:** active members, in spaces that switch on member self-service.
