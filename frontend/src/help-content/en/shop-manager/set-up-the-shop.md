Each space runs its own shop: kits, consumables and merchandise, collected in person. Every feature of it is optional.

## Switch it on

1. The Space Manager opens **Settings**, then **Modules**, and turns on **Shop**.
2. Under **Features**, choose what you need: online orders, guest checkout, member prices, tax, returns, cash-ups, pickup at events, order limits, bundles, reorder suggestions.
3. Under **Users**, give someone the **Shop Manager** role.

![Screenshot: the shop features under Features](screenshot:shop-features)

## Shop settings

Set the returns window (14 days by default), the pickup note, and your policy text. Holds keep stock for 15 minutes during online checkout and 48 hours for pay-at-desk orders. Uncollected orders get reminders and are cancelled after 30 days unless you change it.

Every sale goes through the finance ledger. Shop money is seen by the Space Manager, the Shop Manager and finance staff.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager switches the shop on and assigns the role; the Shop Manager runs the shop's own settings.
