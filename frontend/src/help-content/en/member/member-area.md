Your member area lives at `/member` (on a shared site, `/m/<space>/member`). Its tabs follow what the space runs, so you may not see all of them.

## The tabs

- **Home**: what's on loan, what's overdue, your bookings, anything that **Needs your attention**, your activity and your check-ins.
- **Loans**: **Out now** for what you have, **History** for past requests and returned items.
- **Bookings**: your upcoming and past room bookings, and **Book a space**.
- **Events**: **Going**, **Past** and partner events, with your tickets.
- **Payments**: what you owe, what you've paid and your receipts. See [Pay what you owe](help:member/pay-what-you-owe).
- **Inbox**: notices from the space, announcements and where you get notified. See [Inbox and notifications](help:member/inbox-and-notifications).
- **Account**: your profile, member card, certifications, invitations and channels.

![Screenshot: the member area Home tab with the On loan, Overdue and Bookings figures](screenshot:member-area-home)

## Find something you did

1. Open **Loans** and choose **History** to see every request and returned item.
2. Open **Events** and choose **Past**, or **View event history** for the full list.
3. Open **Payments** for every charge and receipt.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with an account. Loans, Events and Inbox need an active membership where the space runs memberships; Payments always shows, so you can settle anything you owe.
