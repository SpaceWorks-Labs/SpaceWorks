## Give

1. Open **Support** on the space's page (`/donate`).
2. Under **How much**, pick a suggested amount or type your own.
3. Under **What it goes to**, choose a general donation, a campaign or a fund.
4. Under **How often**, choose **Once**, **Every month**, **Every quarter** or **Every year**.
5. Not signed in? Fill in **About you**, press **Email me a code** and enter the code.
6. Ask for a tax-exempt receipt if the space offers one and you need it.
7. Press **Continue to payment**, **Give** or **Make my pledge**.

![Screenshot: the Support this space page](screenshot:member-donate)

A pledge raises a charge each period; no card is stored. Without online payment, you pay the space directly and it sends a receipt. A tax number you enter is stored encrypted and only its last four characters are ever shown.

## Your gifts and pledges

1. Open **My gifts** on your member Home, or the private link in your email.
2. Check the status: **Waiting for payment**, **Received** or **Cancelled**.
3. Press **Pay online**, or **I've paid** if you paid another way.
4. Download your tax-exempt receipt when it's issued.
5. For a pledge, change the amount or how often and press **Save changes**, or **Pause**, **Resume** or **Cancel pledge**.

Keep emailed links private: anyone with the link can see the gift or change the pledge.

> **Who can do this:** anyone, members and guests alike.
