## Send a job

1. Open **Request a job** (`/machine-jobs/new`).
2. Choose a **Machine type** and press **Next: files**.
3. Add your design files, plus screenshots if they help. Press **Next: details**.
4. Fill in the title, material, number of copies and, if you need it by a date, **Needed by**. Press **Next: confirm**.
5. Tick the three confirmations and press **Send request**.

![Screenshot: the Request a job form on the files step](screenshot:member-job-request)

## Follow it

Open **My machine jobs** from Home. Each job shows where it is in the queue and an estimated time.

- **A quote arrives**: press **Approve quote** or **Decline**.
- **Prepaid machine types**: press **Pay now** after approving.
- **Ready to collect**: show the **Pickup code** at the desk.

Collect within 14 days, or the item becomes the space's. If a run fails through the space's fault, it's re-run for free.

> **Who can do this:** active members who are checked in and have accepted the waiver. Where the space requires training, you need a valid certification for that machine type; see [Your certifications](help:member/certifications).
