## "Are you still coming?"

1. Open **More**, then **Broadcasts**, then **Are you still coming?**
2. Press **Ask everyone not yet asked**.
3. Watch the counts: asked, still coming, not coming, no answer yet.
4. Press **Remind people who have not answered**.

A "no" frees the place. For a paid place, staff assess any refund under the event's policy; it is never automatic.

## Re-accept a declined registration

Open **Registrations**, find the rejected row and press **Re-accept**. If a price applies, the row shows that payment is due; it's raised as a normal charge.

## Crew

1. Open **More**, then **Crew**.
2. Under **Sign-up link**, tick **Open the crew sign-up link**, and **Review each sign-up before approving** if you want to vet people.
3. Share the link, or press **Add to crew** for someone you know.
4. **Approve** or **Decline** sign-ups waiting for review.
5. Check **Catering** for numbers and dietary answers people gave the event.
6. Optionally show crew who opted in on the event page.

![Screenshot: the Crew panel](screenshot:em-crew)

Crew places are free and never use the event's capacity.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers, the Space Manager, and helpers granted registrations (attendance) or volunteers (crew).
