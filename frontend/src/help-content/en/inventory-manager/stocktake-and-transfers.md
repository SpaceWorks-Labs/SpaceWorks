## Run a stocktake

1. Open **Stocktake** and press **Start count**.
2. Choose a container, and whether counters see the expected number (**Show expected**) or count blind.
3. Count the shelves and enter what you find.
4. Press **Complete count**, review the differences, then **Approve** and **Apply adjustments**.

![Screenshot: a stocktake in progress](screenshot:im-stocktake)

Approving changes the inventory numbers to match the count. Count rules and a schedule are under **Count policy and schedule**, set by the Space Manager.

## Move stock

1. Open **Transfers** and press **New transfer**.
2. Choose where it's coming from and going to: a space and a container on each side.
3. Add each product and quantity, and a reason.
4. Press **Move stock**.

> **Who can do this:** Inventory Managers and the Space Manager.
