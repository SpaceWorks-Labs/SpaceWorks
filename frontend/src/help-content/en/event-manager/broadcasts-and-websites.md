## Email registrants

1. Open **More**, then **Broadcasts**, then **Email registrants**.
2. Write a subject and message, choose which registrations, and add up to three calls to action.
3. Press **Save draft** or **Save and review**, then send.

## Event website

Under **More**, **Website**, edit the sections and release them. To show the event on a partner's site, add their exact `https://` address under **Event embeds** and copy the snippet.

## Short link

Under **Settings**, **Publishing**, **Short link**: press **Create short link**, then **Copy link** or **Download QR PNG**. **Make a new link** stops the old one at once. Visits are counted per day, never who visited.

## Co-hosts and partners

Under **More**, **Team**:

- **Organizers & partners**, then **Save organizers**.
- **Partner makerspaces**, then **Invite a partner makerspace**.
- **Participating organizations**, to let an organisation's members register.
- **Staff access**: invite a helper by email with exactly the permissions they need. They accept and work from **Assigned resources**.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers and the Space Manager.
