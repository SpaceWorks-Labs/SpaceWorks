## Order statuses

- **Checkout in progress**: the buyer is paying; stock is held.
- **Pay at the desk**: held for the buyer to pay in person.
- **Paid**, then **Ready for pickup** once you've packed it, then **Collected**.
- **Paid, out of stock**: paid but you can't fill it; open a refund case.
- **Cancelled** and **Refunded**.

![Screenshot: the shop orders list with statuses](screenshot:shop-orders)

Orders are picked up at the space, or at an event where event pickup is on. Nothing is shipped.

## Cancel

Cancelling an unpaid order moves no money and puts the stock back.

## Returns

1. Open the order and choose the items coming back.
2. For each, choose **Back to stock** or **Written off**.
3. A refund case opens for the money.

Shop money is only ever refunded through a staff-approved refund case. See [Refunds](help:finance/refunds).

> **Who can do this:** the Shop Manager and the Space Manager; staff with the shop view can read orders.
