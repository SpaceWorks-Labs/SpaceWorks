The space's **Inventory** page lists everything it lends, with live availability.

## Ask to borrow

1. Choose **Inventory** from the space's home page. Search, or pick a category.
2. Press **+** on each item you need. It goes into **Your request** (on a phone, **Review request**).
3. Describe what it's for under **Request purpose**.
4. If the space asks, choose **When will you collect?**
5. Press **Submit request**. You see **Request submitted**.

![Screenshot: the Inventory page with the Your request card](screenshot:member-borrow-request)

Staff review each request. When it's accepted, collect it at the desk: staff scan the box and take a photo as they hand it over. Some spaces let an uncollected request expire after its pickup window.

## Your borrowing history

Open **Your history** from your member Home or **Account**, then choose **Borrowing history**.
It lists your requests, staff handouts, self-checkout loans and returns in this space, with their
actual dates, states and item outcomes. Use **All borrowing**, **Requests and current loans** or
**Closed requests and returns**, then **Next** and **Previous** to reach older records.

This read also works for an account without membership while loans are switched on. It never shows
another borrower's records, private evidence or stock locations. **Payments & receipts** opens
your separate money history, including charges recorded without online payments.

![Screenshot: the account-owned borrowing history with a returned request](screenshot:member-borrow-history)

## When an item is out

- **Join the waitlist** puts you in line ("You're number 2 in line", for example). When it's **Ready for you**, press **Request it**. The item is held for you until the time shown.
- **Notify me when back** just emails you when it's back.
- **Leave** gives up your place.

## Change your mind

Open **Loans**, then **History**, and press **Withdraw** on a pending or accepted request. Anything held for you goes back on the shelf.

## Renew

1. Open **Loans**, then **Out now**.
2. Press **Renew loan**. You see the new due date.

You can't renew if someone else is waiting for the item or your membership has lapsed.

## Returning

Bring it back to the desk. Staff take a return photo and a note before the loan closes. Returns are never refused.

> **Who can do this:** active members (or any signed-in account where the space has no memberships). You need the space's waiver accepted, and some spaces also need you checked in.
