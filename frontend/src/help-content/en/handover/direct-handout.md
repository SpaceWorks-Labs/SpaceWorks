## Hand a tool over

1. Open **Direct handout** and press **New handout**.
2. **Borrower card**: press **Scan member card**, or choose the borrower.
3. **Scan or select tools**: scan each tool's QR, or set quantities for counted items.
4. Take the issue photo and add a remark.
5. Press **Confirm handout**, and **Print receipt** if wanted.

![Screenshot: the guided direct handout on the Scan or select tools step](screenshot:ho-direct-handout)

## Take it back

1. Open **On loan** and press **Return** on the loan.
2. **Check items**, then take the photo and scan the tool's QR where it needs one.
3. Add return notes and press **Submit return**.

**Claim codes** lists codes borrowers can use to link a handout to their account later.

> **Who can do this:** staff who can make direct handouts (Handover, Inventory Manager, Space Manager). A handout needs a photo and an eligible tool; a return needs a photo and notes.
