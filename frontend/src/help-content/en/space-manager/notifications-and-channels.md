## Staff alerts in a chat room

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Under **Rooms**, press **Add a room**.
3. Pick the **Channel** (Slack, Mattermost, Discord, Telegram or a signed webhook) and give the room a name.
4. Paste the room's chat ID or webhook address.
5. Optionally narrow **Deliver to this room** to certain machines or categories, then press **Save room**.

![Screenshot: the Add a room form](screenshot:sm-add-room)

A room with nothing selected gets every alert. Webhook addresses are stored encrypted and never shown again. Only chat modules you've installed appear.

## Staff email and recipients

Under **Notifications**, switch on **Send staff emails** and choose **Notification recipients** for each area manager.

## Channels for members

Under **Communication channels**, press **Turn on** for a channel, enter its details, and press **Send test**. Members then choose it in their inbox. Chat apps ask each member to opt in once.

## Email delivery and templates

- **Email delivery** holds your sender address, or your own mail server.
- **Email templates** lets you edit the wording of the emails Space Works sends.
- **Delivery logs** shows every message sent, including ones a switched-off module skipped.

## Your own inbox

**Inbox** in the console holds staff notices for your space. **My channels** sets where you personally are notified.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager sets up rooms, channels and email. Every staff member uses their own inbox and channels.
