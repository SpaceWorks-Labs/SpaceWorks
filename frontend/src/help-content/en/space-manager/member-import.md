## From a file

1. Open **Members**, then **Import**, and press **Import members**.
2. Choose a CSV, TSV or Excel file (up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows).
3. Under **Match your columns**, tell Space Works which column holds what.
4. Press **Preview**. Each row shows what will happen, such as an email repeated in the file.
5. Press **Import**, then **Download result CSV** for your records.

![Screenshot: the import preview with row outcomes](screenshot:sm-member-import)

## From a Google Sheet

Where the space has switched it on:

1. In Google Sheets, press **Share** and set **General access** to **Anyone with the link · Viewer**.
2. In **Import members**, choose **Google Sheets link** under **Import from**.
3. Paste the link into **Sheet link** and press **Preview**.
4. Check the columns and the preview, then press **Import**.

Only shared-by-link sheets work; Space Works never signs in to Google. It reads the first tab (or the tab in your link), with column names in row 1, and keeps nothing. If the sheet changes after the preview, preview it again.

## What happens next

Each person gets an email invitation and becomes a member when they accept. Nobody is linked to an existing account on their behalf. In a space without accounts, rows are kept as records only.

> **Who can do this:** anyone holding the members action, once member import is switched on.
