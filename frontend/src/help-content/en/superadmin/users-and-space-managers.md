Only you give, change or remove the Space Manager role.

## Give someone the Space Manager role

1. Open **Users** (shown as **Staff & roles**) and pick the space.
2. Use **Grant roles** for an existing member, or **Invite by email**.
3. Tick **Space Manager** on its own; it can't be combined with other roles.
4. Press **Save roles**.

![Screenshot: Staff & roles with the space selector](screenshot:sa-users)

A space always keeps at least one active Space Manager, and nobody manages their own roles.

## Other staff actions

**Change roles**, **Revoke staff roles** and **Reset authenticator** (with a reason) work for any staff member in any space.

## Create a space

Press **Create makerspace**. On a multi-organisation site it needs an owning organisation.

> **Who can do this:** the platform superadmin. Names and emails stay masked until you open a personal data window.
