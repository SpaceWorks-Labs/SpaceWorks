## Book

1. Open **Book a makerspace** (`/bookings`), or **Book a space** in your Bookings tab.
2. Pick a resource. Its label says whether it **Needs approval** or is **Instant when available**.
3. Press **Choose a time** and set the start and end.
4. Press **Book this slot**, or **Submit booking request** for one that needs approval.

![Screenshot: a resource page with Choose a time](screenshot:member-book-slot)

Times show in your device's time zone. A priced booking is charged once it's confirmed.

## Your confirmation and arrival QR

1. In **Bookings**, open **My bookings** and press **View confirmation**.
2. Add it to your calendar with **Google Calendar** or **Download calendar**.
3. Use **Download printable pass** or **Download QR PNG**.
4. Show the QR when you arrive. Staff can scan it from 30 minutes before the start.

Keep the QR private. **Replace private QR** makes a new one if it leaks.

## Change or cancel

- **Reschedule booking**: set a new start and end, then **Save new time**.
- **Booking actions**, then **Cancel booking** and **Confirm cancellation**.
- After your slot, **Check out** and tell the space how it went.

You can only change a booking before its cut-off. A refund for a paid booking is decided by staff.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with an account; members need an active membership and the waiver. Recurring bookings are set up by staff.
