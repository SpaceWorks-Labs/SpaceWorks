## Find an event

1. Open **Events** on the space's page, or **Discover** to see events across spaces.
2. Search, or filter by **Free events**, **Registration open**, a category or a tag.
3. Open an event to read the details, **Add to calendar** or **Share event**.

## Register

1. On the event page, press **Register** (or **Join waitlist**, or **Apply** when places are chosen by the organisers).
2. **Choose a ticket** if there are several.
3. Fill in an invitation code if the event is invite-only, accept the host's waiver, and add a promo code if you have one.
4. Submit. You see **Registration received**, **You're on the waitlist** or **Application awaiting approval**.

![Screenshot: an event page with the Register button](screenshot:member-event-register)

You need an account; there is no guest sign-up. You're only charged once your place is confirmed.

## Your ticket

1. In your **Events** tab, press **Open ticket**.
2. Show the check-in QR at the door, or **Download ticket PNG**.
3. Add it to your calendar or **Open the map**.

The QR appears once your place is confirmed. To cancel, press **Cancel registration** before the event's deadline.

## After the event

- **Leave feedback** from your past events.
- Find your certificates under **Certificates** in your **Events** tab, where the organisers issue them. Press **Download PDF** (or **Prepare PDF** first), or **Download image (PNG)**.
- Invited to speak? Open the speaker link, sign in and press **Claim speaker invitation**.

> **Who can do this:** anyone with an account. Members-only events need an active membership and the space's waiver.
