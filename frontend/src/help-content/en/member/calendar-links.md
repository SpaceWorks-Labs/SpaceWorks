A calendar link keeps your calendar app up to date with what you've booked or signed up for.

## Create a link

1. In your member area, open **Events**, then **Calendar links**.
2. Choose **My bookings calendar**, **My shifts calendar** or **Everything in one calendar**.
3. Press **Create link**.
4. Copy it straight away; it's shown only once.
5. Paste it into your calendar app's "subscribe" or "add by URL" option.

![Screenshot: the Calendar links cards](screenshot:member-calendar-links)

## Keep it safe

Treat a private link like a password: it shows your own items in this space. If it leaks, press **Rotate link** for a new one or **Revoke link** to stop it.

The **Public events calendar** link lists the space's public events and can be shared freely.

> **Who can do this:** members, volunteers and mentors, for whatever the space has switched on.
