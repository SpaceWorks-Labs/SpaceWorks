## Scan an arrival

1. Open the space, then **Arrivals**, then **QR arrival**.
2. Scan or paste the booker's QR.
3. Check the details and press **Confirm arrival**. Press **Scan another** for the next.
4. Got it wrong? **Reverse recorded arrival**. At the end, **Record check-out**.

![Screenshot: the QR arrival scanner](screenshot:bm-arrival)

Recording an arrival doesn't complete the booking and doesn't open any doors.

## Design the pass

1. Open the space, then **Pass designer**.
2. Choose paper, orientation and card size.
3. Choose the fields: name, resource, booking window and reference.
4. Press **Download sample PDF**, then save the layout.

Passes never show a booker's custom answers.

> **Who can do this:** Booking Managers, the Space Manager, and resource staff granted check-in or details editing.
