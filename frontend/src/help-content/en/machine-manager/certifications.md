## Create a certification

1. Open the machine type, then **Certifications**, and press **New certification type**.
2. Name it and set how many days it's valid (blank means it never expires).
3. Tick whether it's **Required for service requests** and **Required for bookings**.
4. Press **Save certification**.

## Train someone

1. Press **View grants**, choose the member and press **Grant certification**.
2. Later, **Renew** or **Revoke** it, with training notes or the reason.

![Screenshot: a certification's grants list](screenshot:mm-certifications)

Members see their trainings under **Certifications** in their account. Without one, a job request or booking for the type is refused.

## Reminders

Where certification reminders are on, set them under the type's **Settings**, **Reminder policy**. Members are told before expiry, and staff get a digest.

Deactivating a certification type stops it gating anything; training records stay. A staff booking can override the rule with a reason, and every override is audited.

> **Who can do this:** Machine Managers for their types, and the Space Manager.
