## Add a product

1. Open the shop's catalogue and add a product.
2. Add variants for each size or colour, each with its own SKU, price and stock.
3. Set a reorder point so you know when to restock.
4. Where member prices are on, set the member price too.
5. Publish it.

![Screenshot: a shop product with its variants](screenshot:shop-product)

## Stock

Stock moves through sales, returns and the counts you record. It's kept apart from what you lend, unless you link a product to a consumable where that's switched on. Where reorder suggestions are on, items below their reorder point are suggested for the To-Buy list. Public pages show **In stock**, **Low** or **Sold out**, never the exact count unless you allow it.

## Price changes

A buyer whose order is still open sees the new total before paying. Where price-drop refunds are on, lowering a price offers the difference back to people who already paid.

> **Who can do this:** the Shop Manager and the Space Manager.
