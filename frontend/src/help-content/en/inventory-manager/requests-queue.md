**Requests** holds every borrow request in queues: **Pending review**, **Ready for handover**, **Active loans** and **History**.

## Review a request

1. Open **Requests**. **Pending review** is first.
2. Search if you need to, then press **Review** on a request.
3. To accept, press **Accept**. You can lower any line, or set it to 0 to decline just that item. Press **Accept** again to confirm.
4. To reject, press **Reject**, give a **Reason** the member will see, and press **Reject request**.

![Screenshot: the Accept request dialog with editable quantities](screenshot:im-accept-request)

Accept at least one unit. Accepting reserves the items, so they show as unavailable to others until handed over or released.

## Due dates and cancelling

From a request's menu, **Set due** then **Save due date**, or **Cancel request** if it won't go ahead.

## What happens next

Accepted requests move to **Ready for handover**. See [Hand over a request](help:inventory-manager/issue-a-request).

> **Who can do this:** Inventory Managers and the Space Manager. Front-desk staff see the handover queues but not **Pending review**.
