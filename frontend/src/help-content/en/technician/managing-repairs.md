## Triage and assign

1. Open **Repairs**, then **Queue**. Filter **Open**, **New reports** or **Fixed and closed**.
2. **Triage** a member's report and set its priority, or press **New repair job** for something you found yourself.
3. Press **Send to technicians** (the area's usual technicians are pre-ticked), or **Assign to me**.

![Screenshot: the Repairs queue with New reports](screenshot:tech-repairs-queue)

## Add technicians

1. Open **Technicians** and press **Add technician**.
2. Choose **Someone on staff** or **Outside contractor (no account)**.
3. Add their email, phone or chat, how they get repair requests, and what they cover. Press **Save**.
4. For chat, press **Send chat opt-in link**. They agree once on the page it opens; the link lasts seven days. Email always works.

## Quotes and payouts

1. On a job with a quote, press **Approve quote** or **Decline quote**.
2. When the work's paid for, press **Record payout**: amount, how, reference and date.
3. **Close** the job.

A payout is recorded in the finance ledger; no money moves from this screen.

> **Who can do this:** the managers of each area (machines, inventory, rooms) and the Space Manager. Only the Space Manager sets a Technician's coverage.
