## Record a return

1. Open **Requests**, then **Active loans**, and press **Record return**.
2. Scan the box and check the borrower as at handover.
3. For each item, choose **Returned**, **Damaged** or **Missing**.
4. Take the return photo and write the **Return remark**.
5. Press **Confirm return**.

![Screenshot: the return step with Returned, Damaged and Missing](screenshot:im-return)

You can return part of a loan: items marked **Not returned now** stay on loan.

## Damaged or missing items

Damaged units go to the [To-be-fixed shelf](help:inventory-manager/to-be-fixed). Missing items stay on the borrower's record and show in [Accountability](help:inventory-manager/ledger-and-accountability). If the space charges for damage or loss, a charge step follows and staff set the amount.

> **Who can do this:** staff who can take returns (Inventory Manager, Handover, Space Manager). A return always needs a photo and a remark, and at least one item resolved.
