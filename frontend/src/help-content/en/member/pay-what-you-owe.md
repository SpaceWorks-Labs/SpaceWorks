Everything you owe a space is in the **Payments** tab of your member area: bookings, event places, machine jobs, membership dues, deposits and late fees.

## Pay

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Press **Pay now** on a charge to pay by card or the space's online payment.
3. A charge that says **Pay at the space** has no online payment: pay at the desk and staff record it.

![Screenshot: the Payments tab with a charge to pay](screenshot:member-payments)

## Pay by UPI

Where the space offers it:

1. Scan the QR, or press **Open UPI app**, and include the note shown.
2. Enter the **Transaction reference (UTR)** and the date you paid.
3. Press **I've paid**.

Staff check that the money arrived and confirm your payment.

## Receipts and refunds

- **Payment documents**, then **Show documents** and **Download PDF**.
- To ask for money back, press **Request refund**, say why and press **Send request**. Staff decide every refund; a loan deposit is returned when the loan closes, not through this button.

## A pay link

Staff can send a pay link for a charge, even to someone without an account. Open it and pay; it expires after a while.

> **Who can do this:** anyone who owes a charge, with or without a membership. "Nothing to pay. You are all settled." means you're clear.
