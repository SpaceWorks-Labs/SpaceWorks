# User manuals

Every role has a manual in the in-app **Help** guide (`/help`). The guide shows the sections that match your role and hides articles for modules and features your space has switched off. The **Help** link beside a staff screen opens the article for that screen. A custom role can cover several areas; read each area's section. The articles below are the same English source files the app renders.

Built-in roles: Space Manager, Inventory Manager, Machine Manager, Event Manager, Booking Manager, Handover, Technician, Shop Manager and Member. Finance, organisation, platform superadmin and host-operator sections cover work done through actions, organisation seats or platform access rather than one built-in role.

## What changed for users in 0.11.0

- **Inventory has its own page.** A makerspace home is an overview of its enabled areas; tools to borrow live on **Inventory** (`/m/<space>/inventory`, or `/inventory` on a single-space site). See [Getting started](../frontend/src/help-content/en/member/getting-started.md) and [Borrow tools](../frontend/src/help-content/en/member/borrow-tools.md).
- **Light mode by default.** A visitor's saved light or dark choice is kept. See [Public site and branding](../frontend/src/help-content/en/space-manager/public-site-and-branding.md).
- **Configurable public stats.** The Space Manager controls the page; Inventory, Event, Machine and Booking Managers can be granted control of their own area's figures. See [Public site and branding](../frontend/src/help-content/en/space-manager/public-site-and-branding.md#public-stats-and-responsible-managers) and [Lending rules](../frontend/src/help-content/en/inventory-manager/lending-settings.md#choose-what-visitors-see-in-public-stats).
- **One banner and logo.** The listing banner also heads the makerspace home, and an uploaded logo replaces the default mark in the public header. See [Public site and branding](../frontend/src/help-content/en/space-manager/public-site-and-branding.md#name-logo-and-colours).
- **Shop pickup.** Orders are collected at the space or an event; nothing ships. See [Set up the shop](../frontend/src/help-content/en/shop-manager/set-up-the-shop.md), [Orders, pickups and returns](../frontend/src/help-content/en/shop-manager/orders-and-returns.md) and [Buy from the shop](../frontend/src/help-content/en/member/shop.md).
- **Listing photos.** Items, events, booking resources, machines and shop products each have a photo control, shown on their public pages where the record is published. See [Items](../frontend/src/help-content/en/inventory-manager/inventory-and-categories.md), [Create an event](../frontend/src/help-content/en/event-manager/create-an-event.md), [Resources](../frontend/src/help-content/en/booking-manager/resources.md), [Machines](../frontend/src/help-content/en/machine-manager/machines-and-types.md) and [Products](../frontend/src/help-content/en/shop-manager/products-and-stock.md).
- **Your own history.** Members and account-only borrowers open **Your history** for borrowing, events, bookings, shop orders and payments. See [Your member area](../frontend/src/help-content/en/member/member-area.md#find-something-you-did).

## Space state and switched-off areas

Suspension pauses ordinary service. Read-only mode keeps permitted reads available while blocking changes. Archive keeps the space's records and pauses its ordinary workflows. The platform superadmin manages these states through **Spaces**. When an action is missing or refused, check both your current role and the space's state.

Use the [switch-off reference](MODULES-OFF.md) to check what a disabled module or feature hides and what it keeps, and [logging and audit](logging-and-audit.md) to investigate a recorded action or a failed request.
