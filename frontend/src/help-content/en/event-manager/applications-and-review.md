Any event can take applications instead of open registration. Hackathons use the same tools.

## Collect and decide

1. Turn on applications for the event and set up the form under **More**, **Programme setup**.
2. Read applications under **Applications**.
3. Stage your decisions (offer, waitlist, decline) with **Stage private decisions**.
4. Press **Preview capacity**, then **Release this batch**.

Applicants see a decision only once its batch is released. A paid place is charged only after it's confirmed.

## Review in stages

Where review stages are on, under **Applications**, **Review stages**:

1. Press **Add a stage**. Set up to ten criteria, guidance and a review window. Tick **Blind review** to hide names and identifying answers. Press **Add stage**.
2. Under **Panel and assignment**, choose the **Reviewer panel** and press **Save panel**.
3. Under **Assign reviewers**, choose **Balanced workload** or **Random**, press **Preview**, then **Apply this plan**.
4. Press **Open for review**.
5. When reviews are in, tick applications and press **Advance selected to the next stage**.

![Screenshot: the Review stages planner](screenshot:em-review-stages)

Reviewers work in **Application reviews** and see only their own stage and assignments. A stage's criteria and blindness freeze once it has reviews. Without stages, applications get one review round.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers, the Space Manager and helpers granted application review.
