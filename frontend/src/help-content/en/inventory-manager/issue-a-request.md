Every handover leaves evidence: a box QR scan, a photo and a note. Space Works won't issue without them.

## Guided handover

1. Open **Requests**, then **Ready for handover**, and press **Start handover**.
2. **Scan container**: scan the QR on the box you're handing over.
3. **Check borrower card**: scan or look at the member's card and tick that you checked them against the request.
4. **Check items**: confirm each item. Tools with their own QR are scanned one by one with **Scan items continuously**.
5. **Photo and confirmation**: take the issue photo, add a remark, and press **Confirm issue**.
6. Press **Print receipt** if the borrower wants one.

## A broken unit at handover

In the quick form (**Assign + issue**), use **Reject as broken** on a unit and choose where it goes: the To-be-fixed shelf, or out of inventory.

> **Who can do this:** staff who can issue requests (Inventory Manager, Handover, Space Manager). You can never issue without a box scan and an issue photo, or issue more than was accepted.
