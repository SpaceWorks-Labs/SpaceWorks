Every staff role needs two-step sign-in: an authenticator app or a passkey. Member features never need it.

## For new staff

1. Sign in to the staff console. A banner shows the date your grace period ends.
2. Press **Set it up now**.
3. Choose **A passkey** or **An authenticator app**, and follow the steps. See [Two-step sign-in and passkeys](help:member/two-step-sign-in).
4. You return to the console.

![Screenshot: the staff banner asking to set up two-step sign-in](screenshot:staff-mfa-grace)

You have three days. After that, staff screens are blocked until you set it up; your member area keeps working.

## When someone loses their device

1. They use a recovery code to sign in, if they saved one.
2. Otherwise, open **Users**, find the person and press **Reset authenticator**. Give a reason.
3. They enrol again at their next sign-in.

> **Who can do this:** every staff member enrols. The Space Manager (or the platform administrator) resets a staff member's authenticator.
