# Equipment kits and contextual requests

Inventory staff can create a kit from equipment and consumable items, publish it for eligible requesters, and create a new version when its contents change. Publishing, archiving and editing are separate actions. If another staff member changes a kit first, review the latest version before trying again.

When requesting equipment, a signed-in person may choose individual items or one published kit. They may also select their own event registration, their own exact booking, both, or neither. A selection explains the purpose; it does not approve a request or issue equipment. Event and booking staff can propose equipment for the person on their authorized registration or booking; inventory staff retain the separate review and handover actions.

Returnable items in a kit follow the usual loan, evidence and return workflow. Consumables in a kit are planned separately: creating the plan does not use stock. Authorized inventory staff record actual use through the existing consumables workflow. A consumable-only kit creates a plan rather than an equipment loan.

If a submission's outcome is uncertain, use **Retry the same request** while the page retains it, or review your authorized request history. Do not create a replacement until the first outcome is known. Changing accounts clears the private pending form; returning after a reload requires checking history.
