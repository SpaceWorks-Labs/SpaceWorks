## Hand over a job

1. Open **Job handover** (the page is **Ready to collect**).
2. Check **Waiting** and **Unpaid** at the top, and search by name.
3. Check the payment pill on the job: **Paid**, **Unpaid** or **Waived**.
4. Press **Hand over job** and type or scan the person's **Pickup code**. Leave it blank if you know them and are handing it over in person.
5. Press **Mark collected**.

![Screenshot: the Ready to collect list](screenshot:ho-job-pickup)

Where the space asks people to pay before pickup, an unpaid job can't be handed over: settle it in Payments first.

> **Who can do this:** the Handover role and Machine Managers.
