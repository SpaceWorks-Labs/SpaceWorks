## Order statuses

- **Held for you**: online checkout is in progress; stock is held when holds are enabled.
- **Awaiting payment**: the buyer pays at the desk.
- **Paid**, then **Ready to collect** once you've packed it, then **Collected**.
- **Could not be filled**: payment arrived but stock could not fill the order; staff review the refund case.
- **Cancelled**: the order was closed without collection.
- **Returned**: all goods were taken back. This status does not confirm that money was refunded.

![Screenshot: the shop orders list with statuses](screenshot:shop-orders)

Orders are picked up at the space, or at an event where event pickup is on. Nothing is shipped.

The buyer's order page shows the recorded payment, ready and collection times, plus returned items and their values. A signed-in buyer opens **My shop orders** and can follow **Payments, refunds and receipts** to their own money history. A guest follows the private order link in their email; that link does not grant access to an account or another order.

## Cancel

Cancelling an unpaid order moves no money and puts the stock back.

## Returns

1. Open the order and choose the items coming back.
2. For each, choose **Back to stock** or **Written off**.
3. A refund case opens for the money.

Shop money is only ever refunded through a staff-approved refund case. A return records the goods and the value for review; actual refund progress and money paid back come from the payment ledger. Existing returned-item history remains visible if taking new returns is switched off. See [Refunds](help:finance/refunds).

> **Who can do this:** the Shop Manager and the Space Manager; staff with the shop view can read orders.
