A **module** is a whole area, such as Bookings, Events or Machines. A **feature** is a finer switch inside one, such as recurring bookings or event promo codes. Switching anything off hides it and keeps its data; switching it back on brings everything back.

## Modules

1. Open **Settings**, then **Modules**.
2. Flip a module's switch on or off. Core modules say **Always on**.
3. If it needs other modules, the row says **Also installs** and lists them; they're switched on with it. A toast lets you **Undo**.
4. A module another one depends on says which to turn off first.

![Screenshot: the Modules page with grouped module switches](screenshot:sm-modules)

## Features

1. Open **Settings**, then **Features**.
2. Tick or untick a feature under its group.
3. A feature whose module is off says to turn the module on first.

Area managers also find their area's switches in their own settings dialogs (**Inventory settings**, **Machine settings**, **Event settings**, **Booking settings**) when you've given them control. Security switches live on the [Security](help:space-manager/security-settings) page instead.

## Deleting data

Switching off never deletes. Purging a module's data is a separate step only the platform administrator can take, with a preview first.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager. Modules with a usage limit on a hosted plan are installed by the platform administrator.
