This guide is on for every space by default. It shows each reader the sections that fit their role, and every staff console screen has a **Help** link that opens the matching article in a new tab.

## Who sees what

- Everyone reads the member and organisation sections.
- Staff see the sections for the roles they hold. You, as Space Manager, see every staff section, so you can train your team.
- The platform administrator also sees the superadmin and host-operator sections.
- Articles about features your space hasn't switched on are hidden from your space's guide.

## Turn it off

1. Open **Settings**, then **Features**.
2. Untick **Help centre** under **General**.

![Screenshot: the Help centre switch under Features](screenshot:sm-help-centre-switch)

The Help links disappear from the console and your space's help pages stop opening. Nothing is lost: tick it again to bring them back.

> **Who can do this:** the Space Manager.
