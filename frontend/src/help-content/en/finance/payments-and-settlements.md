**Payments** lists every charge your space has raised: what's to collect, what was paid online or in person, and what was waived or cancelled.

## Record money taken in person

1. Open **Payments**. Filter **To collect**.
2. On a row, press **Mark paid**. For several at once, select them and press **Mark paid in person**.
3. In **Record payment**, choose the method (cash, UPI, bank transfer, card machine, cheque, other), add a reference and the date received.
4. Press **Save receipt**.

![Screenshot: the Record payment dialog](screenshot:fin-record-payment)

You record what happened; no money moves. Every receipt is kept, and a mistake is fixed with **Correct receipt**, which adds a correction rather than changing the original.

## Waive a charge

**Waive** cancels a charge that shouldn't be paid. It needs the authority for that area.

## Pay links

Where pay links are on, press **Pay link** on a row, optionally add an email, and **Create link**. It pays that one charge, even for someone without an account. Links last 14 days by default; a new link cancels the old one.

## Two-person check

Where the two-person check is on, a receipt someone records waits under **Finance**, **Offline payments**, until a second person with the payments action presses **Confirm** (or **Reject**). Nobody confirms their own receipt.

> **Who can do this:** staff who record payments, and the managers of each area for their own charges; viewing needs the finance view. An Event Manager sees event charges only.
