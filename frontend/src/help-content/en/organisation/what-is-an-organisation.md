An organisation is a shared profile for a group that works with one or more spaces: a college club, a company, a community group. It has its own people and its own public page, and it can run events through the spaces it's linked to.

## What it can do

- Hold **people**, with owners and governors who run it.
- **Link** to spaces it works with. Linking needs approval from the platform.
- Publish a **public page** at `/o/<organisation>` listing its linked spaces and events.
- Run **events** through a space it owns, and see attendance across its spaces.
- Issue **ID cards** through a space's ID programmes, where the space offers them.

![Screenshot: an organisation's public page](screenshot:org-public-page)

## Who does what

- **Owners and governors** edit the profile, invite people, and see the organisation's console.
- **Members** of the organisation can register for events opened to it.
- **The platform administrator** approves links between organisations and spaces.

An organisation grants access to actions, never to identity: belonging to one never shows you anyone's personal details.

> **Who can do this:** anyone can read a public organisation page. Running one needs an owner or governor seat.
