1. Open **Repairs**, then **Maintenance**.
2. Pick a row marked **Overdue** or **Due today**.
3. Fill in **What was done**, and parts and notes if any.
4. Press **Log maintenance**.

![Screenshot: the Maintenance view with due rows](screenshot:tech-maintenance)

The next due date is set from the schedule. A machine that was locked out for overdue maintenance unlocks once it's logged.

## What you cover

The Space Manager sets your coverage: certain machine types, all items, or named rooms. With no coverage, you only see the repair jobs assigned to you.

> **Who can do this:** Technicians within their coverage, and the area managers.
