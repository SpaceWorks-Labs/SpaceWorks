## Issue and manage cards

1. Open **Member cards**.
2. Press **Issue a card**, find the member under **Members without a card** and press **Issue card**.
3. In the **Cards** list, use **Reissue card** or **Revoke card** (with a reason). Revoked cards stay listed for the record.
4. **Print all active** prints every active card; the **All cards** menu also offers a PDF, a ZIP or emailing everyone their card.
5. **Scan a card** checks a card at the desk.

![Screenshot: the Member cards list with Print all active](screenshot:sm-member-cards)

Bulk printing and emailing run in the background; watch the job list for **Ready**.

## Settings

Under **Settings** in Member cards, choose whether to **Issue a card when a membership starts**, **Email the card when it is issued**, and whether cards have a **Public check page**.

## Design the card

1. Open **Card design**.
2. Pick the space default under **Design**, or add a design for one plan or member title.
3. Press **Start from a template** for a ready layout (Classic, Bold, Minimal, Community, Student or Event pass).
4. Use **Add** for text, data fields, the photo, your logo or QR codes; **Layers** and **Background** for the rest. The font library includes many scripts.
5. Press **Test print** and print the PDF at 100% to check the size.
6. Press **Publish**, or **Submit for approval**.

Stay inside the dashed safe area. Cards keep the last published design until you publish a new one. A member's card uses their title's design, then their plan's, then the space default.

## Approving designs

When approval is on, a staff member's design waits as **Waiting for approval**. The approver opens it and presses **Approve and publish**, or **Send back** with a note.

> **Who can do this:** staff who manage members or member cards issue and design cards; the Space Manager approves designs. Staff who only scan cards see just the scanner.
