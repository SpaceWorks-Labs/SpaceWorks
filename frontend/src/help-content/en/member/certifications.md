Some spaces ask you to be trained before you use a machine type, book a room linked to it or send it a job.

## Check your training

1. Open **Account** in your member area.
2. Choose **Certifications**.
3. Each training shows **Valid**, **Expired** or **Revoked**, with its end date.

![Screenshot: the Certifications tab with a valid training](screenshot:member-certifications)

When a training is close to its end date, the screen asks you to ask staff to reassess and renew it. Without a valid training, a job request or booking for that machine type is refused with a reason.

> **Who can do this:** active members. Staff record and renew training; see [Certifications](help:machine-manager/certifications).
