## Read your notices

1. Open **Inbox** in your member area.
2. **Notices** holds messages about your own loans, bookings, jobs and payments. Switch between **Unread** and **All**, or filter by family.
3. Press **Open** to go to the thing it's about, or **Mark read**. **Mark all read** clears the list.
4. **Announcements** holds messages the space sends to all members.

![Screenshot: the Inbox tab with notices](screenshot:member-inbox)

## Choose where you're notified

1. Open **Inbox**, then **Channels**.
2. Under **Your channels**, add the ways you want to hear from the space. Chat apps such as Telegram or WhatsApp ask you to opt in once with a link.
3. Under **What goes where**, choose which kinds of message go to which channel.

## Choose what you're sent

Under **Preferences**, untick the optional messages you don't want, or bundle them into a **Daily digest**. Safety and account-recovery emails always stay on.

> **Who can do this:** active members of spaces that run notifications.
