Spaces with memberships give each member a card. It works on your phone, and you can print it.

## Show your card

1. Open **Account** in your member area and choose **Card**, or press **View your member card** on Home.
2. Press **Show QR to scan** and hold your phone up to the desk scanner.
3. Press **Save card for offline use** so it works without a signal.

No card yet? The page says so; ask the front desk to issue one.

## Name and photo

1. Set **Name printed on the card** and press **Save name**.
2. Under **Card photo**, tick the consent box and press **Upload photo**.
3. Press **Preview card (PDF)** to check it.

## My cards

Where the space runs the newer card wallet, **My cards** (`/member/cards`) collects every card you hold: your member card, ID programme cards and event cards.

1. Open **My cards**.
2. Switch between **Ticket** (the QR the desk scans) and **Printed card**.
3. Use **Download PDF**, **Download PNG** or **Email me my card**.
4. Open **Card settings** to choose **Show my name** and **Show my photo** on the public check page, or add emergency details that print on your card only.

## The check page

Anyone who scans your card's check QR sees whether it is valid, who issued it and until when. Your name and photo appear only if you switched them on. **Make a new check link** stops the old QR from working, so print the card again afterwards.

> **Who can do this:** active members once staff issue a card. A revoked card stays invalid until staff issue a new one.
