**Repairs** shows the jobs assigned to you: broken machines, items and rooms.

## Work a job

1. Open **Repairs**, then **My jobs**, and open a job.
2. Press **Start work**.
3. As you go, use the job's menu: **Log a visit** (date, who came, what happened, photos), **Add a remark**, **Part used** (taken from stock), **Part needed** (added to To-Buy), or **Record a quote** (amount and who quoted).
4. When it works again, press **Mark fixed**, say what was fixed and add photos.

![Screenshot: a repair job with Start work and the job menu](screenshot:tech-repair-job)

The person who reported it is told it's fixed. You see the reporter's first name only.

## Quotes

A quote you record waits for the area manager or the Space Manager to approve it before the work goes ahead. You never handle payment.

> **Who can do this:** Technicians, for jobs assigned to them; managers see every job in their area. Technicians can't edit inventory, money or settings.
