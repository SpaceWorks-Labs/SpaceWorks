When an outside group runs an event at your space, you can charge them a venue fee.

1. Open the event, then **More**, **Tickets & invites**, **Venue fee**.
2. Enter the organiser's account and, optionally, the room (its venue price applies).
3. Choose the price: **Room or space price**, **Free** or **Custom**.
4. For a custom price, set the amount and whether it's charged **Once** or **Per hour of the event**.
5. Press **Save venue fee**.

![Screenshot: the Venue fee for hosting form](screenshot:em-venue-fee)

The fee is charged when the event is published, or straight away with **Charge now**. It's charged once and the amount is frozen; after that the form locks. A zero fee raises nothing.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers and the Space Manager. The charge appears in the organiser's payments and in your finance ledger.
