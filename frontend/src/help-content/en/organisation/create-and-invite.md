## Create one

1. Open **Account** in your member area and find **Create your organization**.
2. Fill in **Organization name** and **Organization slug** (the short name in its web address).
3. Press **Create private organization**, then **Manage your organization**.

Where self-creation is off on this site, ask the platform administrator to create it for you.

## Invite people

1. Open the staff console's **Organizations** tab and pick your organisation.
2. Open **Invitations** and press **Invite someone**.
3. Add an email if you want to tie the invitation to one person, and choose how many days it lasts.
4. Press **Create invitation**, then **Copy link** and send it.

![Screenshot: the Invitations tab with Create invitation](screenshot:org-invitations)

The person opens the link (**Join an organization**), signs in and presses **Sign in and continue**. Each link works once; revoking it stops it at once.

## People and spaces

The organisation's tabs are **Profile**, **Public page**, **People**, **Invitations** and **Spaces**. Under **People**, use **Suspend** or **Reactivate**, and grant or revoke governor seats.

## Leave

1. Open **Account** and find **Your organizations**.
2. Press **Leave** next to the organisation.
3. Tick that you understand your access will end, and press **Leave organization**.

A sole owner must appoint another owner first.

> **Who can do this:** verified account holders create organisations where the site allows it; owners and governors invite and manage people; any member can leave.
