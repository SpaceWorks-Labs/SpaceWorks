## At the door

1. Open the event, then **Check-in**.
2. Press **Scan online**, then **Check in by QR** and scan the ticket.
3. Press **Confirm attendance**, then **Scan another**.

![Screenshot: the Check-in tab with the scanner](screenshot:em-check-in)

Check-in opens once the event is published. Waitlisted people can't be checked in.

## Without a connection

Where offline check-in is on: press **Download roster** before the doors open, **Scan offline** at the door, and **Synchronize** when you're back online. Keep the page open meanwhile.

## PIN stations

**PIN check-in station**, then **Create PIN**, lets a shared device check people in. **Rotate PIN** signs every station out.

## Printing badges at the desk

Where badge printing at the desk is on, a window opens after each check-in with the person's real badge.

1. Press **Print badge** or Enter. The badge prints at its true size on the paper size set in the badge design.
2. **Print again** reprints it; **Skip** or **Done** closes the window. **Edit design** opens the badge designer (not shown on PIN stations).
3. If your browser says the print window was blocked, allow pop-ups for this site and print again.

This works offline too: the downloaded roster carries each badge, and the prints are reported when you **Synchronize**.

To print many badges, open **Badges**, choose who (confirmed, attendees without crew, crew, checked in, not yet checked in, or everyone) and the paper: one badge per page for card and label printers, or A4 or Letter sheets with crop marks. Waitlisted and pending people print as provisional badges without a check-in code.

To collect from a check-in list at the door, choose the list in the **Desk** menu above the scanner. Scans then record a collection, with a beep and an Undo for a minute.

## Check-in lists

For things handed out during the event (swag, meals, a workshop seat), where check-in lists are on:

1. Open **More**, then **Check-in lists**, and press **New list**.
2. Name it, choose the kind, and who qualifies: everyone with a place, or buyers of an add-on. Optionally only after entry.
3. Press **Create list**. At the table, press **Collect**, then **Record collection**.
4. **Archive** it when done.

These lists never change entry check-in, and they work online only.

> **Who can do this:** Event Managers, the Space Manager and helpers granted check-in.
