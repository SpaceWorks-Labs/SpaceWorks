Two-step sign-in asks for something only you have after your password. It is optional for members and required for every staff role.

## Set up an authenticator app

1. Open **Account & two-step sign-in** from your user menu, or go to `/mfa`. The page is called **Account security**.
2. Confirm your current password.
3. Press **Enroll authenticator**, then **Show enrollment QR and key**.
4. Scan the QR with your authenticator app. If you can't, open **Can't scan? Enter this key**.
5. Type the six-digit code and press **Verify**.
6. Save your recovery codes with **Copy all codes** or **Download codes**, then press **I saved my codes. Continue**.

![Screenshot: the Account security page with the authenticator section](screenshot:member-mfa-enrol)

Each recovery code works once. Keep them somewhere safe and offline.

## Add a passkey

A passkey lets your phone or computer confirm it's you with a fingerprint, face or PIN.

1. On **Account security**, find **Passkeys** and press **Add a passkey**.
2. Confirm with your device.
3. Use **Rename** to tell your passkeys apart, or **Remove** to delete one.
4. Next time, choose **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in screen.

Adding or removing a passkey signs you out on your other devices. Passkeys are never required. If a space switches them off, you sign in with your authenticator and your saved passkeys stay listed.

## Signing in with the second step

After your password, enter the code from your app and press **Verify**. You can also choose **Use a passkey instead** or **Use a recovery code instead**.

## Remembered devices

**Remembered devices** lists the browsers that passed an extra sign-in check. **Forget** removes one; its next sign-in simply asks for the check again. A remembered device never skips two-step sign-in.

> **Who can do this:** every account holder. Staff must set up an authenticator or a passkey; see [Two-step sign-in for staff](help:space-manager/staff-two-step).
