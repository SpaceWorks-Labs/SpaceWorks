1. Open **Organizations**, then **All organizations**.
2. **Create organization**: a name and a public slug.
3. Link or unlink spaces. Changing an organisation's reach asks you to confirm.
4. Manage **Invitations** (**Create invitation**, **Copy link**) and people (**Suspend**, **Reactivate**).
5. **Delete organization** works only once it has no linked spaces.

![Screenshot: All organizations](screenshot:sa-organisations)

Organisations grant actions, never identity. A space hidden from the platform can't be reached through an organisation.

> **Who can do this:** the platform superadmin; organisation governors manage their own.
